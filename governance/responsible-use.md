# Responsible use, collaboration, and representation

**Status:** proposed community expectations; AI-assisted; author review pending.  
**Not a new license or a contract with every reader.** Existing permissions and legal exceptions remain unchanged.

## The distinction

People can learn from, critique, adapt, and develop work under its actual permissions without becoming project members or representatives. Root Sequence's wish for humane outcomes is not a claim to control other people's independent thought.

The expectations below describe the cooperation the project seeks. For already CC0-covered material they are requests, not newly enforceable copyright conditions. Where a collaboration needs enforceable obligations, negotiate an appropriate express agreement before sharing additional unpublished material. See [licensing](licensing.md).

## Requested practice

- **Preserve context and credit.** Identify sources and relevant limitations; label adaptations. Do not present an exploratory question as an established finding, or AI-assisted draft text as reviewed human testimony.
- **Respect agency and consent.** Do not use publication as an excuse to expose participants, infer consent to unrelated uses, or conceal manipulative, discriminatory, coercive, or surveillance applications. Assess harms to people beyond the original creator.
- **Avoid misleading representation.** Do not imply Root Sequence endorsement, affiliation, sponsorship, certification, or authority to speak for its contributors without authorization. Accurate reference, criticism, and lawful nominative use are different from claiming an endorsement. This is not a claim of registered trademark rights.
- **Make collaboration reciprocal.** Discuss contributor credit, maintenance, benefit-sharing, payment where labor is requested, publication review, access, exit, and dispute handling. Benefits need not be only commercial, and there is no automatic royalty or hiring entitlement for publicly licensed reuse.

## Before an institutional collaboration

Record the actual scope and parties; distinguish already-public work from unpublished contributions; agree on deliverables and payment; identify background work and any newly commissioned work; define confidentiality, internal access, retention, model-training/use permissions where relevant, publication review, attribution, and authorized representation. Preserve previously granted public rights and the rights of other contributors. Do not transfer broad rights through an ambiguous intake form or submission portal without reviewing its terms.

An organization's nonprofit status or stated mission is not a substitute for checking the proposed conduct and agreement. Equally, commercial status alone is not evidence of misuse.

## A concern or suspected misuse

1. Preserve exact public passages, URLs, revisions, dates, and the relevant terms privately. Do not gather unrelated personal information or use covert tracking.
2. Separate resemblance or influence from copying, breach of an agreement, misleading endorsement, privacy harm, or an unlawful act. Record uncertainty and possible independent development.
3. Choose a proportionate response: request a correction or credit, clarify non-endorsement, seek a discussion, stop optional future collaboration, or seek qualified advice where concrete rights or harm are at issue.
4. Use the existing [security route](../SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities and the [private concern route](../contributing.md#choose-the-right-contribution-path) for sensitive non-security matters. Do not post allegations or confidential evidence in a public issue by default.

No guaranteed response time, surveillance program, automated accusation, or legal enforcement service is established here. A takedown request should not be used merely to prevent lawful learning from ideas.
