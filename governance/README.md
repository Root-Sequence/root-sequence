# Research stewardship

**Status:** proposed operational guidance; AI-assisted draft; author review pending.  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-22.  
**Scope:** publication, reuse, provenance, collaboration, and the boundaries of this research commons.

Root Sequence should remain useful to people without treating openness as an obligation to disclose everything. These proposals distinguish what the project publishes, what its licenses actually permit, what collaborators expressly agree to, and what it asks people to do voluntarily.

**Learning from a commons does not confer authority over it.** Equally, lawful independent learning, criticism, commercial use permitted by a license, or parallel discovery is not automatically exploitation.

## What this does not do

This is not a new license, a retroactive restriction, a contract with every reader, or a promise to control downstream ideas. The existing [CC0 dedication](../license), applicable file-specific terms, third-party rights, and law remain distinct from these proposals. Citation requests cannot turn CC0 into an attribution-required license. See [licensing](licensing.md).

The proposals do not establish a governing body, represent other projects' consent, or change repository visibility. Public drafts remain public. The word `draft` means not adopted; it does not mean confidential.

## Relationship to existing work

[Project Stewardship](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/.github/blob/main/PROJECT_STEWARDSHIP.md) owns maintenance and documentation propagation. The [commons](../commons/README.md) owns broader commons practice and proposals. This directory owns the narrower publication/reuse procedure, not a competing account of the whole project.

Project-specific safety, consent, archive, contributor, and release rules remain authoritative within their scope. Where rules conflict, surface the conflict and obtain a decision rather than silently overriding it. Do not copy private working records into public governance files.
