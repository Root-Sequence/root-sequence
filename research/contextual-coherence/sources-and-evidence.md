## Unresolved work

Domain review must determine which variables and obligations matter in a real setting. A transport application needs contemporary local evidence, current legal/engineering review, underlying-study appraisal, affected-party involvement, and a justified evaluation protocol. Governance research needs stronger evidence on capture, unequal capacity, participation costs, cross-boundary enforcement, and remedies. These are open investigations, not commitments to run a study or modify a street.
