# Contextual coherence: design and research questions

**Status:** Proposed method and unexecuted comparisons / AI-assisted / author and domain review pending (2026-10-29). This companion operationalizes [Coherence Without Uniformity](README.md); it is not an approved governance protocol, participant study, or traffic-engineering specification.

## A reviewable rule record

Use a proportionate record rather than making every choice an administrative project. Before a consequential local variation, make these fields inspectable:

| Field | Required question |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and protection | What capacity or harm does the rule address? Which protections bind it? |
| Boundary | Where, when, and to whom does it apply? Who outside that boundary is affected? |
| Mechanism and context | Why should the intervention work here? What relevant differences and uncertainties remain? |
| Alternatives | What is the competent common-rule baseline? What less burdensome or less coercive alternatives exist? |
| Participation and power | Who could shape the options, decide, object, and appeal? Who was absent or underrepresented? |
| Authority | Who may propose, authorize, implement, enforce, pause, amend, and retire it? Which external obligations require checking? |
| Material means | Who funds, maintains, communicates, monitors, and repairs it? Is that capacity actually available? |
| Interface | How do newcomers learn it? What happens at boundaries, during outages, or when neighboring rules differ? |
| Outcome and distribution | What counts as improvement, unacceptable harm, or unresolved evidence, and for whom? |
| Revision and repair | What triggers review? Who can suspend it? What fallback, expiry, repair funding, and renewal process exist? |
| History and privacy | What version, reasons, objections, evidence, and decisions are retained? What personal information is unnecessary? |

Public reasons need not expose complainants. Preserve a meaningful audit trail while limiting collection, access, retention, and secondary use of personal data. An objection should not require a public life history.

## Revision procedure and its own revision

The working sequence is **proposed → scoped → reviewed → authorized → implemented → assessed → retained, revised, suspended, or retired**. These are record states, not software already implemented or a universal required order. Urgent protective action may need a narrower temporary path with subsequent review.

1. **Receive a mismatch or objection.** Record the claimed consequence and urgency, including whether someone cannot safely use the existing complaint route.
2. **Identify the level of change.** Action, operational rule, goal/protection/boundary, or amendment procedure. Do not treat authority at one level as authority at every level.
3. **Investigate and develop alternatives.** Use [Epistemic Contrast](../methods/epistemic-contrast.md), domain methods, affected perspectives, a competent baseline, and explanations that could contradict the preferred intervention.
4. **Make a decision through the relevant authority.** Preserve dissent, unresolved uncertainty, conditions, resource commitments, and the reasons for rejecting options. Consultation, a research finding, or an AI recommendation is not authorization.
5. **Communicate and implement a versioned change.** Record the actual operative behavior, scope, effective date, responsible actors, dependencies, fallback, and appeal route. A revised statement without changed practice is not enough.
6. **Observe and assess.** Compare expected with observed consequences, distribution, burdens, displacement, missing reports, and explanations other than the rule change.
7. **Act on the assessment.** Retain, modify, stop, repair, or retire with reasons. Make unresolved findings available to the next review.

The process itself needs scrutiny. Can a person propose a new measure, add an excluded group, challenge a reviewer, or appeal the body's refusal to reopen a rule? Who can alter these powers? A revision to the amendment procedure should name its beneficiaries, affected groups, new powers, and removed checks; it requires separate authorization and cannot be hidden in an operational update.

Do not hard-code continuous revision as a virtue. Stable guarantees and predictable review windows can reduce burden. An emergency exception needs a duration or renewal condition, a review owner, and a documented end state. Loss of monitoring should activate a declared safe response, not indefinite permission to proceed.

## Comparative hypotheses

**None of the following comparisons has been executed for this inquiry.** They are directional proposals that could be narrowed or rejected. Measures, thresholds, observation periods, recruitment, analysis, and authority must be agreed before any real study.

| Hypothesis | Predicted observation | What would reverse or narrow it? | Adverse outcome to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1: Context-responsive operational rules can outperform a common procedure where relevant conditions differ. | Compared with a competent common-rule design, fewer access failures or lower burdens in the specified contexts, with protection thresholds maintained. | No meaningful contextual interaction; costs exceed benefits; a well-designed common accommodation performs as well or better. | A minority loses access while average journey times improve; harms move to neighboring areas. |
| H2: Effective affected-party influence improves detection and correction of omissions. | Independently identified omissions appear in proposals or enacted changes sooner, with practical access to review across groups. | Improvements reflect expertise, funding, or facilitation rather than influence; no enacted response; excluded groups remain unrepresented. | Participation fatigue, intimidation, disclosure pressure, or capture by well-resourced participants. |
| H3: Limited common interfaces make local variation easier to use. | Fewer consequential boundary errors or repeated administrative steps than equally resourced variation without those interfaces. | Shared presentation hides materially different obligations; comprehension does not improve; maintenance costs outweigh gains. | Visitors act on a false expectation, or compatibility requires excessive identity/data sharing. |
| H4: Funded, bounded revision powers improve correction after a mismatch. | A justified change reaches operative practice sooner, with preserved protections and lower unrepaired burden than advisory feedback alone. | Delay was appropriate; original rule was sound; resources rather than revision authority explain the result. | Rule churn, emergency powers without expiry, rushed changes, or complaints used to suppress less powerful groups. |

## Comparison design

Start with document analysis or constructed cases when exposure would be inappropriate. For a bounded model comparison, hold actors, resources, shocks, information quality, and baseline protections constant while varying rule scope, decision powers, or interfaces. Vary these separately before attributing an effect to the entire package.

Include at least these comparators:

- a competent common standard with appropriate accommodations and equal resources;
- contextual variation with effective participation and defined interfaces;
- the same variation with advisory-only consultation, to distinguish having a voice from having influence;
- a stable original rule facing a false or irrelevant complaint, where retaining it may be correct;
- a mismatch with no authority or implementation resources, to separate recognition from capacity;
- a context shift and an externality crossing the chosen boundary.

A deliberately bad uniform baseline cannot establish the value of adaptation. A positive result under invented assumptions is model behavior. Empirical transport work needs suitable designs and professional assessment; human-participant work needs its own accessible protocol, oversight, consent, burden limits, and withdrawal provisions. Civic participation is not study enrollment.

## Observations, stopping, and reversal

Maintain distinct measures for safety, access, reliability, comprehension, administrative work, maintenance, privacy, distribution, and the ability to challenge a rule. Do not combine these into a single coherence or legitimacy score. Identify who remains unobserved, who stopped participating or traveling, and how reports may be distorted by fear, selection, publicity, or enforcement.

For a proposed transport comparison, specify exposure by travel mode and route, relevant time windows and conditions, observation methods, uncertainty, and spillover boundaries. Investigate serious harm, near conflicts, failed yielding, inaccessible crossings, journey abandonment, and delay distributions. Define any proxy carefully; near-miss counts and self-reports do not substitute automatically for crash-risk estimation or lived access.

Before implementation, set unacceptable exposure limits, review thresholds, reporting routes, and a responsible pause authority. Leave numeric thresholds **undecided until justified in the particular domain**; this document does not supply invented engineering criteria. An unresolved serious access or safety concern may require pausing even if the aggregate target is met. A short observation period or missing reports may warrant no conclusion.

Reversal requires a feasible alternative, responsible operator, money, time, communication, dependency accounting, and a way to repair continuing harms. If returning to the old state would recreate harm, the response may need an interim protective arrangement. Retiring an experiment does not retire obligations to people affected by it.

## Return and revision conditions

A useful return records the source revision, domain, actual rule and authority, implementation evidence, observations, distribution, competing explanations, dissent, unintended consequences, and what should be changed or left unchanged. Distinguish a changed policy document from an enacted change and an enacted change from improved outcomes.

The principle's added value should be narrowed if ordinary domain analysis supplies the same decisions more clearly, if the vocabulary obscures accountability, or if the coordination and participation costs dominate. Negative findings belong in the canonical inquiry. A simulation return is a question for further investigation, never automatic authorization for a real rule or project adoption.
