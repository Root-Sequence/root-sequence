## Relationship to the Technical Grant Writer role

The [current employer description](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/aiaf/jobs/6205780004), checked 2026-10-17, asks for translating incomplete research into proposals with clear objectives, methods, milestones, and warranted claims. It explicitly permits effective use of AI tools while requiring accuracy, judgment, and final quality. Those criteria guide sample selection; they do not determine Root Sequence's mission.

The first sample demonstrates conceptual synthesis and proposal structure. It does **not** by itself demonstrate technical fluency with ML methods or AIAF's current technical research agenda. A later sample should explain a technical paper's actual method, assumptions, evidence, and one bounded extension after appropriate reading and review. Employer-specific fit and final application wording remain separate decisions.

## Handoff requirements

Each selected sample needs an exact revision, source locators, inspection scope, contribution disclosure, unresolved claims, and the reviewer's actual decisions. Rae's review can proceed through short passages and source comparisons, with notes and assistance available. Do not substitute unaided recall, polished delivery, or agreement among AI outputs for checking a claim.
