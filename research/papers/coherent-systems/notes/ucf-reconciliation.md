## Sources and definition map

[UCF's reconciliation record](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/universal-coherence-framework/blob/c4d7b236828a59139c0d5204b1faaed13154682a/docs/reconciliation-2026-09-16.md) compares the older internal/external/temporal definition, the glossary's integration language, Root Sequence's model/reality account, and C-003's fit/legitimacy distinction. Its [v1.1 candidate](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/universal-coherence-framework/blob/c4d7b236828a59139c0d5204b1faaed13154682a/models/ucf-model-v1.1-draft.md) owns the proposed model revision. Original UCF wording remains available in exact snapshots; v1 is unchanged.

The paper now distinguishes functional performance, coherence, and normative legitimacy. Following author review, target execution alone is treated as functional performance, not coherence. Apparent fit that depends on coercion, suppressed dissent or feedback, hidden externalized costs, or excluded affected people is provisionally described as **false coherence**. Concealment is not required; acknowledging coercion may improve legibility without repairing the coerced relationship or restoring agency. The term has existing UCF provenance in manufactured-consensus and coherence-hijacking treatments, but this broader application remains a synthesis for review rather than a validated measurement procedure.

UCF's internal, inter-agent, systemic, and temporal layers are different from Chaos, Tension, Flow, and Unity. The paper needs no universal state progression. Whether either structure adds useful information is part of the research question.

## What the example establishes

The [canonical booking example](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/universal-coherence-framework/blob/c4d7b236828a59139c0d5204b1faaed13154682a/models/examples/booking-service/README.md) implements a specified finite capacity problem. Its rules distribute access differently while leaving the same amount of requested service work unmet in the base fixture. Sufficient capacity removes the modeled exclusion. These results follow from constructed assumptions, not observations of a service or proof of a preferred rule.

The four layers organize the questions. The state labels supply no demonstrated added explanation in this editorial application. A competent ordinary resource-and-impact review could identify the same facts; T-003 must test added value fairly.
