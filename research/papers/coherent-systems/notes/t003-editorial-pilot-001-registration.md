## Method A — competent ordinary incident/system review

Use only the frozen packet.

Produce no more than **12 findings**. For each finding provide:

- finding;
- packet evidence/basis;
- why it matters;
- proposed action;
- uncertainty / missing evidence.

Review the case as a competent senior reliability/system reviewer. Specifically:

1. reconstruct the main failure and propagation chain;
2. identify contributing design, implementation, dependency, deployment, monitoring, incident-response, and recovery factors supported by the packet;
3. distinguish prevention, detection, containment, recovery, and verification actions;
4. identify where one protective/recovery mechanism worsened or failed to contain another failure;
5. identify missing information needed before prioritizing major changes;
6. separate packet-reported facts from analytical inference.

Do not use Root Sequence/UCF terminology unless it is already present in the packet.

Do not produce an overall score.

## Method D — frozen dynamic-coherence review

Use the exact 13-question prompt in the frozen T-003 protocol.

Produce no more than **12 findings**. Each finding must include:

- evidence/basis;
- affected component/party;
- consequence;
- timescale;
- uncertainty;
- proposed design/research implication.

Do not produce an overall coherence score or method comparison inside the Method D output.

## Excluded methods

- STPA: excluded from Pilot 001 because method-fidelity review has not been completed.
- Capability-oriented check: excluded because human substantive opportunity is not central to the selected technical incident.

## Reviewer / conflict statement

Both Method A and Method D outputs are generated by the same AI-assisted research process that helped develop the dynamic-coherence framework.

This is a major conflict of interest.

Therefore Pilot 001 can test:

- whether prompts are usable;
- whether findings can be coded;
- obvious duplication;
- obvious unsupported extrapolation;
- whether the comparison protocol needs revision.

It cannot establish superiority, novelty, reliability, or independent agreement.

## Output constraints

- Evidence packet only.
- Maximum 12 findings per method.
- No external factual additions.
- Unsupported assumptions must be marked.
- Missing information should be requested, not invented.

## Burden measurement in this pilot

Human reviewer preparation time and analysis time are **not measurable** in this AI-only run and must be reported as unavailable.

Use only:

- prompt question count;
- output character/word count;
- number of findings;
- additional-information requests;

as crude editorial-burden indicators.

Do not interpret these as human usability measurements.

## Null result

A valid null result includes:

> Method D's decision-relevant findings are all or nearly all already captured by Method A, with no clear additional decision value relative to its added conceptual overhead.

## Retirement signal

If repeated independent comparisons show the same pattern, dynamic-coherence review should be narrowed into routing guidance among established methods rather than promoted as a distinct review method.
