### Purpose and audience

Develop a bounded, examinable argument about how intelligent systems interact with their surrounding institutions, infrastructure, material environments, and affected people. The audience includes researchers, designers, builders, and technically literate general readers.

The paper is a synthesis within Root Sequence, not the charter of a new institution or a replacement for the ecosystem's independent projects.

### The question for v0.1

**What must be examined beyond the objectives of an individual agent to assess whether a change improves the conditions of the wider system?**

The proposed contribution is a review method connecting system boundaries, assumptions, consequences, the sources and limits of decision-making power, material constraints, and revision over time. It now explicitly draws on UCF's four-layer model without requiring its four-state vocabulary or assuming universality. Whether that method adds anything to existing sociotechnical alignment and systems practice is an open research question, not a novelty claim.

### Scope and non-goals

v0.1 should define its terms, locate prior work, develop one counterexample to local-to-systemic inference, specify a small assessment procedure, and propose tests.

It will not claim to solve society, predict AGI, prove inevitable progress, provide a universal coherence score, replace technical AI safety, or establish one final social arrangement. Intelligence is neither presumed benevolent nor presumed malevolent. Removing a monetary constraint is not the same as removing material, ecological, coordination, maintenance, or consent constraints.

### What “living” means

Substantive changes pass through review, update the claims ledger and changelog, and retain contrary evidence. Git history records revisions; it does not supply scholarly validation. This scaffold does not configure automatic rewriting, background research, or deployment.

Development versions use `v0.1-dev`. A reviewed release should identify its date and exact commit. A later citation should identify the title, approved authors, version, date, and immutable revision. No DOI or author endorsement should be invented.

### Evidence and editorial rules

- Separate definitions, literature-supported arguments, inferences, hypotheses, and design commitments. A normative commitment is not an empirical discovery.
- Cite external factual claims with Markdown footnotes; record source coverage and version in the source map and bibliography. Abstract review is not full-text review.
- A counterexample can defeat a universal guarantee without establishing how common the failure is. Scenarios and fiction generate questions, not empirical validation.
- Keep dissent, privacy, legitimate decision rights and enforcement powers, and real constraints visible. Coherence must not become a synonym for obedience or whatever the author prefers.
- Do not copy private notes, personal data, unpublished fiction, or story canon into this public directory. AI-assisted wording remains subject to author revision; the author list is unresolved until approved.
