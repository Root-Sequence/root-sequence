# Drafting and author review

**Requested workflow, recorded 2026-09-16.** Applies to the paper and its supporting notes. This process does not approve any manuscript claim or authorize publication.

## Work through questions

Retrieve relevant existing project material before asking the author to repeat it. Draft one manageable passage or decision at a time. When an ambiguity changes meaning, scope, claims, values, attribution, or publication, ask before making that decision on the author's behalf.

Default to one substantive question at a time. Supply the short passage, example, or source locator needed to answer. Two or three concrete alternatives can help, but leave room for a mixture, neither, uncertainty, or different wording. Avoid leading questions that make one answer sound like the only reasonable choice. Routine formatting and other reversible editorial maintenance do not need repeated permission.

Accept fragments, examples, corrections, and reactions as input. Label your proposed synthesis as interpretation; check consequential changes rather than treating your formulation as the author's own words. Show what changed and why after an answer. Do not postpone all author input until a large finished draft exists.

## Support review without replacing judgment

Keep the relevant text and source material available during review. Do not require an unaided explanation from memory or an improvised defense as proof of participation. Help develop a written explanation that the author can inspect, correct, qualify, or reject. Pause or restate a question when needed.

Source checks and independent evaluation remain necessary where the claim requires them. Do not confuse conceptual agreement with source verification, approval of wording, or approval to publish. A request to continue, silence, or approval of one passage does not approve the entire manuscript. No personal health disclosure is required to use this workflow.

## Keep the record usable

Maintain a brief decision note in the existing claim, source, or change record: author-stated intent, assistant interpretation, affected passage, unresolved question, and review status. Record only decisions actually made. Do not create a new tracking system or ask the author to maintain duplicate logs.

Keep earlier meanings, counterarguments, and source visibility intact. Do not invent earlier contributions, quotations, personal experience, or approvals. Do not publish private conversation excerpts or personal details merely because they explain the process.

Use [AI-ASSISTANCE.md](AI-ASSISTANCE.md) for disclosure and review distinctions and [README.md](README.md) for paper scope. These repository instructions are not a claim that every future tool or conversation automatically loads them.

## Extend what exists and return to the active question

**Author clarification, 2026-09-16:** The aim is to address gaps in existing work, not generate new frameworks by default. Check established literature and existing project treatments, identify the specific missing piece, and add only that piece. Preserve the option that the useful contribution is a synthesis or application, not a novel method. An enthusiastic response to a term is not authorization to create a taxonomy or new project.

Apply [the existing Legible Systems treatment](../../../concepts/legible-systems.md#participation-sensemaking-and-review-burden) to this collaboration: more prose can transfer more review work to the author. Keep enough context for the next decision, avoid expanding unreviewed dependent claims, and provide a short re-entry recap after a research or routing detour. Do not put the burden of restoring context back on the author.

Use [THOUGHT_ROUTING.md](../../../THOUGHT_ROUTING.md) for cross-project routing. Keep open questions in the existing claim record. Neither helpful guidance nor a readable explanation replaces evidence, independent review where needed, or consent to consequential actions.
