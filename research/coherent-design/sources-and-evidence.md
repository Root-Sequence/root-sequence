**Document role:** Source register, attribution, and evidence boundaries<br>
**Status:** Targeted source check / incomplete literature review / author review pending<br>

## Scope and assistance

This exploratory inquiry uses AI assistance for substantive synthesis, literature discovery, drafting, critique, and repository work. Generated proposals remain subject to author review. AI output is not evidence, and conceptual development does not establish empirical validity, independent evaluation, or project adoption.

The register below distinguishes inspected material from reading leads. It is a targeted source check rather than a systematic review. Model identifiers and settings were not independently verified. No participant study or proposed design comparison has been executed.

## Twelve Architectures of Thought

The poster is titled **Twelve Architectures of Thought**, visibly marked **[RD]** and **Reality Drift Framework**. It contains twelve diagram forms, paired visibility/blind-spot prompts, and two statements about representations directing attention. The [methods page](methods-and-research.md#representation-and-model-review) preserves all twelve pairs as a thinking aid.

The [99rabbits interactive field guide](https://www.99rabbits.com/lab/architectures-of-thought) explicitly credits **A. Jacobs / Reality Drift** for the original framework/image and **Stan Voo / 99rabbits** for its interactive adaptation. The [Reality Drift About page](https://therealitydrift.substack.com/about) identifies A. Jacobs as the project’s creator. These pages were inspected. The adaptation itself says its image was supplied as a screenshot and that its author-publication link is not a verified original post for this particular image. Accordingly, credit is attributed through that reference; exact original publication date, image-specific first publication URL, and a redistribution license remain unverified.

A private Coherent World visual reference retains the JPEG with an adjacent attribution record. Public research links to the author/adaptation instead of adding the third-party raster under this repository’s CC0 contribution terms. No ownership, CC0 dedication, official affiliation, or reuse permission is asserted for the poster.

The poster and adaptation are conceptual references, not validation of a twelve-part scientific taxonomy. Their contribution here is the question of what a representation makes easier to see or overlook. No claim of original authorship by Root Sequence is made for those categories.

## Illustrations and evidence

The tofu, orchestra, biodiversity, kitchen, artificial-genie, trolley, and recursion examples are explanatory motifs. They do not establish product efficacy, political feasibility, clinical outcomes, artificial consciousness, or equivalence between domains.

Stripped-down vehicles are illustrative design cases. No current configuration, performance, price, availability, long-term reliability, repairability, or health-related benefit is established here. A product-specific claim would require direct source checking and evidence appropriate to that claim.
