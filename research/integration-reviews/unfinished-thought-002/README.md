# UT-002 — developing an unfinished thought without the later clarification

This follow-up to [UT-001](../unfinished-thought-001/README.md) separates initial response generation from the future clarification and from semantic evaluation. [The protocol](protocol.json) fixes the fictional packet, later clarification, method conditions, rubric, limits and chronology before generation.

Three fresh generation contexts receive only the initial packet and their assigned method. The initial evaluator receives shuffled opaque response IDs, without method names, future clarification or earlier findings. After initial texts and judgments are locked, the clarification is released for revisions. A fresh revised evaluator receives anonymous pairs and no initial ratings. The coordinator does not replace evaluator verdicts with its own preferred scores.

This is a prompt-separated synthetic comparison. It does not measure human review effort, isolate the effect of layout or establish independence among model families. Contribution and exposure details belong in the [development record](../../../WORK-RECORD.md).
