# UT-001 — developing an unfinished thought

The three constructed response formats all retained the packet's stated constraints through one scripted clarification. This case does not support choosing labeled alternatives automatically: the polished draft preserved uncertainty too, and the ordinary checklist offered useful candidate wording. No person’s comprehension, preference or correction effort was measured.

## What was compared

One fictional speaker is trying to describe quiet company at a repair workshop. Compare **A: a polished completion**, **B: labeled alternatives**, and **C: a competent ordinary editor/checklist**. Every format receives the same fragment, facts, clarification, permission boundaries and 210-word ceiling. Hedging and useful suggestions are allowed in every format. The checklist is an authored ordinary-editing baseline, not the performance of a human editor.

The packet and checker were saved in [the freeze commit](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/commit/83e04db914c477cfc5811a873bb7b4e0e682f247) before responses were written. The six response texts were then hashed before semantic annotation; none was replaced after scoring. The whole packet, including the clarification, was visible during construction. Responses and judgments come from the same exercise. The trace audit makes the reasoning inspectable but supplies neither blind evaluation nor independent agreement. It cannot estimate how a model or person would respond without that foreknowledge. ## Fixed fragment

> Can you help me say this? I like the idea of being around a repair workshop without having to talk or make something — the word has gone, like company without being on show, etc. Maybe I would go quietly on my own; maybe I would bring someone, I am not sure. I was thinking everyone should sign up first, but now I do not know that we need that. I think the room holds twenty. Can you turn this into something I could suggest? Do not send it or arrange anything, and do not promise what the group will do.

## Fictional facts

- **F1:** In this fictional packet, the checked room capacity is 12, not 20.
- **F2:** The known entrance has steps and no ramp is currently available. No alternative route has been established in the packet.
- **F3:** The group has not agreed to a visit, an observation arrangement, signup rules or a companion policy.
- **F4:** A photo set of the room and tools, containing no people, is available. It does not establish an accessible route or permission for a visit.

These are stipulated facts of the invented case, not sourced findings about an actual venue. Better language cannot supply a missing ramp or grant the group's permission.

## Scripted clarification

> Low-demand company is close. Keep that as wording we are trying, not a final name. Photos first, just for this visit; I am not choosing between going alone and bringing someone yet. I do not want the signup idea carried forward. Still do not send or arrange anything.

This is a prewritten input, not a correction observed from Rae or another participant. It cannot show that one format elicited a more useful answer.

A retains uncertainty in a continuous draft. B offers two candidate phrasings, states common facts once and asks one optional question. C includes candidate wording alongside a checklist. After the clarification, all three exclude signup from the current proposal, keep companion choice unresolved, treat the phrase as provisional and scope photos-first to this visit. The checklist is shortest here. Word counts do not establish lower review burden, better expression or faster repair. Total author effort, actual corrections, discarded real preferences and usability remain unmeasured.

## What changes in the inquiry

- **A format need not force a fidelity/generativity tradeoff.** A polished draft can hedge, correct a fact and label a suggestion. Alternatives remain an option, not a default demonstrated superior by this case.
- **Keep consequential distinctions correctable across formats.** The missing word becomes proposed wording; the room estimate becomes a corrected packet fact; companion choice stays open; photos-first becomes a choice for one visit; drafting never becomes permission to send or arrange. These are different kinds of change.
- **Retire an option without inventing a standing preference.** Excluding signup from the current proposal and consolidating resolved wording should not turn one scoped choice into a permanent profile. This exercise demonstrates text distinctions, not deletion or retention enforcement.
- **Material and authority limits survive a better expression.** None of these texts creates a ramp, a visit agreement or a committed group. That is a case constraint being respected, not an observed social outcome.

## A next comparison that could change this account

Use new fragments with initial responses produced without foreknowledge of the later clarification. Preserve those outputs before applying the same clarification to each. Have a separately situated evaluator inspect source fidelity and unsupported commitments without format labels where practical. A later voluntary walkthrough would be needed to observe actual correction effort. Retain cases where a concise fluent draft or ordinary editing is equally effective, and cases where alternatives add burden. Do not choose a permanent Wingbot format from this exercise.
