# Conscious Systems

**Document role:** Research-area index<br>
**Status:** Exploratory seed, AI-assisted; author review pending<br>
**Canonical scope:** A bounded inquiry into conditions for subjective experience, across biological and possible artificial systems

> **Guiding question:** What can we change about a mind while preserving consciousness?

The starting intuition is that chemistry, electrical activity, metabolism, connectivity, body, environment, feedback, and history work together in a living cognitive system. The research question is which relationships and processes matter for *having experience*, which shape its contents, and which are features of a particular implementation. That starting intuition is a hypothesis to investigate, not a result or a claim that any system with sufficiently complicated behavior is conscious.

## Boundaries

This area brings neuroscience, anesthesia and sleep research, comparative cognition, philosophy of mind, and artificial-system assessment into conversation. It does not claim to solve consciousness, establish a universal test, infer experience from intelligence or fluent language, or presume that biology is either indispensable or replaceable. The first page is a research agenda; no experiments were run for it.

Related work has different jobs: [emergence in AI](../../systems/emergence/applications/ai.md#capability-agency-and-personhood-are-different-claims) separates capability, consciousness, personhood, and authority; the [coherent systems living paper](../papers/coherent-systems/README.md) asks how intelligent systems interact with their institutions and surroundings. Possible implications for [Coherent Computing](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/coherent-computing), [Coherent World](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/coherent-world), and [Liberated Intelligence](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/liberated-intelligence) belong in those projects when developed. This area owns the general research question.

**Review note:** The guiding question and whole-system intuition came from the initiating discussion. A follow-up author clarification distinguishes experience from memory of its occurrence, memory of its contents, and physical or functional traces that may arise without experience. The structure, literature selection, and proposed tests are AI-assisted interpretations awaiting author review; they are not attributed as approved author conclusions.
