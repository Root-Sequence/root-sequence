## Follow-up analysis specification — not executed

For any future reanalysis, require:

1. A provenance check establishing that available data correspond to this exact experiment, not another paper by the same authors.
2. A participant-flow reconstruction, including exclusion timing and reasons; retain an audit trail for all proposed sensitivity analyses.
3. A condition dictionary that separately labels manipulated settings, behavioral observations, and inferred experiential states.
4. Direct contrasts and uncertainty intervals for the prespecified claim; do not infer a difference from one significant and one nonsignificant test.
5. A justified smallest effect of interest before interpreting a null as evidence against a necessary marker. Do not invent a threshold after observing the estimates.
6. A check of how component windows and electrodes were selected, plus sensitivity to defensible alternative choices. A selection concern is a question to test, not an accusation of invalid analysis.
