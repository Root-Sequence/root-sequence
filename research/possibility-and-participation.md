# Possibility, participation and the conditions of understanding

**Role:** cross-project conceptual synthesis and questions, November 27, 2026. Source and assistance limits are recorded in the [integration source register](integration-reviews/sources-2026-11-27.md). These working phrases do not establish novelty or a common causal mechanism.

What conditions made something possible, and what does it make possible next? Existing work on [adaptive continuity](../concepts/dynamic-coherence.md), [agency and automation](../concepts/agency-and-automation.md), [historical contingency](../analysis/historical-contingency.md), [abandoned futures](../futures/abandoned-futures.md) and [Design Grammar](../concepts/design-grammar.md) already asks substantial parts of this question. The addition here is a comparison of their implications, including where the comparison fails.

## An architecture of possibility, rather than a menu

Selecting an offered option, configuring available arrangements and helping generate new arrangements are different dimensions of agency. They need not form a hierarchy. Delegation can expand agency; mandatory configuration can consume it. **Generative agency** is a working name for participating in the formation of possibilities, including relationships, tools, knowledge and institutions that make later actions feasible.

Resources alone do not describe what a particular person can practically do. Knowledge, skills, permissions, time, bodily circumstances, infrastructure and other people's decisions matter. Affordance research and Sen's capability inquiry are important predecessors, not competing labels to absorb. The proposed conditions → capacities → agency → consequences → changed conditions loop is an analytical prompt. History, chance, other beings and physical constraints interrupt any neat cycle.

## Possibility deprivation: six prompts, not six diagnoses

| Working description | Question before using it |
| --- | --- |
| Concealed | What evidence shows an opportunity existed but was difficult to discover? |
| Inaccessible | Which material, social, sensory, legal or temporal conditions obstruct a known option? |
| Suppressed | Who or what actively prevents development, and what supports that causal claim? |
| Foreclosed | Which recorded decision closed which previously feasible trajectory, for whom? |
| Ungenerated | Which enabling relations are absent? Could a proposed intervention create them? |
| Unimagined | Is this a limit of the investigator's vocabulary, rather than of other people's imagination? |

These conditions can overlap. An unrealized future is not automatically a lost benefit. Claims about prevention and alternative histories need stronger evidence than the plausibility of a story. Closing harmful options, protecting privacy and maintaining dependable commitments can be justified. Do not maximize option counts or convert a counterfactual into an observed loss.

## Participatory existence and the freedom to receive

Being affected by an arrangement differs from having practical influence over it. Participation can concern the framing of a question, interpretation of an expression, amendment of rules or stewardship of infrastructure. It cannot mean everyone's unrestricted influence over everyone else. Expertise, privacy, boundaries, unequal exposure to risk and protection from domination remain relevant.

Opportunity must remain distinct from obligation. Receiving care, enjoying art, resting, using a service or being present need not require immediate contribution. Receptivity names openness to encounter, surprise, learning and being moved; it does not prescribe vulnerability, forgiveness, positive feeling or availability to others. Agency and receptivity can support or undermine each other. Neither demonstrates a being's moral worth, nor establishes artificial subjective experience.

## Ecologies of understanding and imagination

Understanding can change what someone notices and asks next. Imagination can depend on encounters with alternatives, histories, vocabularies, skills, places and other people. This connects [Coherent Communication](../concepts/coherent-communication.md) to discoverability and learning. The broader Ecology of Understanding inquiry retains its exploratory role; this synthesis is not a replacement platform or ownership claim over independent projects.

Prediction asks what may follow specified conditions; imaginative work can vary those conditions. Fiction, music, games, field guides, art and ordinary practice can make a possibility encounterable without proving feasibility or prescribing one future. An ecology of imagination must accommodate incompatible futures, refusal and the possibility that an attractive story hides maintenance, coercion or material costs.

## Tensions to retain and investigate

- **Coherence and productive disorder:** disruption may enable inquiry or destroy essential support. Specify the relation, boundary and timescale rather than celebrating either order or disruption universally.
- **Semantic and coherence debt:** unresolved assumptions and deferred maintenance can transfer work. Treat these as prompts within existing burden/maintenance analysis, not newly measured quantities. Provisional incompleteness can be useful; silent hardening is the concern.
- **Significance and usefulness:** a relation can matter beyond human recognition or exploitation. Changed relevance is not necessarily a changed material or a new phenomenon.
- **Ethics, truth, legitimacy, agency and experience:** coordination is a separate question. Oppressive systems can coordinate; accurate dissent can disrupt them; a coherent narrative can be false.
- **Material and ecological conditions:** energy, water, materials, labor, repair and effects on other life cannot be replaced by attractive governance or interface concepts. Native engineering/ecological evidence is needed.

## Work that could change the synthesis

Use the [Design Grammar](../concepts/design-grammar.md) for patterns rather than create a parallel pattern catalog. Compare a bounded case before and after an intervention: what is available, discoverable, practically reachable, chosen, refused and newly generated? Identify affected actors, costs, authority, observation limits and what would reverse the proposed benefit. Include loss of support, transferred labor, privacy invasion and reduced future capacity among adverse outcomes. A null or a failed cross-domain connection can narrow or retire a working phrase.

The [coverage ledger](integration-reviews/coverage-2026-11-27.md) records routing, not proof that these connections work. Project-owned comparisons may reject this synthesis. Coherence is an inquiry among others, not an authority over autonomous research, humanistic or creative projects.

## Return from the unfinished-thought case

[UT-001](integration-reviews/unfinished-thought-001/README.md) makes one distinction concrete without demonstrating a broader mechanism: proposed language, an available photo set, a choice to view it, practical physical access and permission for a visit are different things. All three constructed response formats preserved those separations. Better expression did not supply the missing ramp or a group agreement. The case therefore supplies no evidence that a more elaborate representation expands practical agency; it clarifies what a later comparison must keep separate.

[UT-002](integration-reviews/unfinished-thought-002/README.md) repeated the case with the future clarification withheld from initial generation and with separate evaluator contexts. Their judgments again supported the distinction between proposed language, a scoped choice, physical access and authority. This is a stronger separation of roles, not evidence that expression changed actual access or that coherence framing adds value.
