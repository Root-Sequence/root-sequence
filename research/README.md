# Research

Root Sequence is an independent interdisciplinary research initiative. This index offers starting points in the research and explains how to read the work’s current evidence and review status.

## Research approach

Root Sequence starts with questions that cross fields. We break them into smaller questions, identify the research and methods that can address each one, and examine how the findings connect.

Differences between fields, competing explanations, and unanswered questions stay visible. Evidence and criticism can change the argument or show that a proposed connection does not hold.

The [research method](method.md) and [method router](method-router.md) contain the full workflow and field-specific routes.

## How to read evidence and status

The source notes should let readers follow a claim back to the work supporting it. They should explain how closely we examined the source and where we have gone beyond it to offer an interpretation.

A test we propose is still an idea until it has been run. When reporting results, we describe how they were obtained, including whether anyone else has independently repeated the work. Unresolved questions remain visible after publication.

The [repository map's labels](../root_map.md#labels-describe-different-things) distinguish document role, maturity, evidence, and canonical scope. A developed document can still contain an unsupported claim.

- **Empirical or formal result:** name the originating study or proof, its scope, and what was actually inspected. A cited result is not a Root Sequence replication.
- **Synthesis or interpretation:** an argument connecting sources, open to competing accounts and domain review.
- **Hypothesis or proposed test:** a question and possible method; not an executed experiment or positive result.
- **Normative commitment or design principle:** a position about what should matter, with its reasons and tradeoffs.
- **Speculation, metaphor, or fiction:** an exploratory form, not evidence that the imagined mechanism works.

Source records distinguish finding a reference from examining it. Reading an abstract or checking selected passages does not amount to appraising the full text. Reproducing an analysis and independently repeating a study also need their own records. Explain what remains uncertain and what could change the conclusion. The [canonical method](method.md) contains the full workflow.

## Accessibility, AI assistance, and contributions

Rae Lovejoy uses AI both as an accessibility aid and as a tool for developing Root Sequence. Ideas often emerge through questions, fragments, observations, and conversations across connected subjects. AI helps gather that material, organize it, explore relationships, compare ideas with existing research, and develop drafts that can be reviewed in manageable pieces.

Rae contributes the motivating questions, conceptual direction, ideas and connections, examples, constraints, objections, and decisions that shape the work. AI contributes substantive synthesis, drafting, critique, literature discovery, and some code and repository work. It also proposes additional ideas, connections, and interpretations that Rae may adopt, revise, or reject. Some ideas develop through this ongoing exchange, so their origins cannot always be cleanly separated.

This assistance supports Rae’s participation throughout development and review. AI output is not evidence, and generated drafts do not automatically represent Rae’s endorsement. Source checking, author review, independent evaluation, and publication approval are recorded separately. The working paper remains a draft with author review incomplete.

### Source and review records

The working paper's [detailed assistance record](papers/coherent-systems/AI-ASSISTANCE.md) preserves the earlier accessibility explanation, actual assistance, and supported review process. Agreement among AI responses is not peer review.

For new work, record the date, tool and model when available, affected artifact, substantive assistance, source locators and inspection scope, and human corrections or approvals. Unrecorded model settings remain unknown. Respect each project's privacy, consent, and publication boundaries.
