## S01 — Cannabis and Frankincense at the Judahite Shrine of Arad

Arie, E.; Rosen, B.; Namdar, D. (2020). Tel Aviv 47(1), 5–28. [Source](https://cris.biu.ac.il/en/publications/cannabis-and-frankincense-at-the-judahite-shrine-of-arad/). DOI: 10.1080/03344355.2020.1732046.

**Inspected:** University bibliographic record and abstract.

**Limit:** Residues and inferred burning at a particular shrine; no general origin claim.

## S02 — The origins of cannabis smoking: Chemical residue evidence from the first millennium BCE in the Pamirs

Ren, M., et al. (2019). Science Advances 5, eaaw1391. [Source](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_3080701/component/file_3080703/content). DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.aaw1391.

**Inspected:** Full main text via Europe PMC XML; results, controls and methods inspected; supplements pending.

**Limit:** Chemical residue evidence; concentrations in the original material and subjective experiences are reconstructed, not observed.

## S07 — Spontaneous and deliberate creative cognition during and after psilocybin exposure

Mason, N. L., et al. (2021). Translational Psychiatry 11, 209. [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8032715/). DOI: 10.1038/s41398-021-01335-5.

**Inspected:** Full main text via Europe PMC XML; task-specific samples and outcomes inspected; supplement access failed.

**Limit:** Later self-rated novelty is not independent originality; convergent thinking remained lower at day seven. Task-specific samples and blinding limit generalization.
