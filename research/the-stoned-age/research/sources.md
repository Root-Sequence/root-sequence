# Source register

Access checked 2026-10-13. Targeted narrative inquiry, not a systematic review. Inspection levels record what was actually accessible. Primary findings require full methods appraisal before stronger claims. A Crossref metadata pass verified 9 records; five requests were rate limited. Other bibliographic checks used the linked publisher, institutional or PubMed records.

## S01

Arie, E.; Rosen, B.; Namdar, D. (2020). [Cannabis and Frankincense at the Judahite Shrine of Arad](https://cris.biu.ac.il/en/publications/cannabis-and-frankincense-at-the-judahite-shrine-of-arad/). Tel Aviv 47(1), 5–28. DOI: [10.1080/03344355.2020.1732046](https://doi.org/10.1080/03344355.2020.1732046).

Inspection: University bibliographic record and abstract.

Boundary: Residues and inferred burning at a particular shrine; no general origin claim.

## S02

Ren, M., et al. (2019). [The origins of cannabis smoking: Chemical residue evidence from the first millennium BCE in the Pamirs](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_3080701/component/file_3080703/content). Science Advances 5, eaaw1391. DOI: [10.1126/sciadv.aaw1391](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aaw1391).

Inspection: Primary paper excerpts, institutional repository.

Boundary: Chemical residue evidence; concentrations in the original material and subjective experiences are reconstructed, not observed.

## S03

Miller, M. J.; Albarracin-Jordan, J.; Moore, C.; Capriles, J. M. (2019). [Chemical evidence for the use of multiple psychotropic plants in a 1,000-year-old ritual bundle from South America](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31061128/). PNAS 116(23), 11207–11212. DOI: [10.1073/pnas.1902174116](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1902174116).

Inspection: Indexed abstract and bibliographic record; direct page retrieval limited.

Boundary: Bundle contents do not establish a specific combined preparation or a participant experience.

## S04

Guerra-Doce, E., et al. (2023). [Direct evidence of the use of multiple drugs in Bronze Age Menorca (Western Mediterranean) from human hair analysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-31064-2). Scientific Reports 13, 4782. DOI: [10.1038/s41598-023-31064-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-31064-2).

Inspection: Full article accessible; abstract, context and methods inspected.

Boundary: Human hair supports exposure; specific social roles and meanings remain interpretive.

## S05

Nichols, D. E. (2016). [Psychedelics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4813425/). Pharmacological Reviews 68, 264–355. DOI: [10.1124/pr.115.011478](https://doi.org/10.1124/pr.115.011478).

Inspection: Review excerpts; receptor-mechanism discussion.

Boundary: Pharmacological classification, not equivalence among all psychoactive substances.

## S06

Griffiths, R. R., et al. (2011). [Psilocybin occasioned mystical-type experiences: Immediate and persisting dose-related effects](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3308357/). Psychopharmacology 218, 649–665. DOI: [10.1007/s00213-011-2358-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00213-011-2358-5).

Inspection: Methods and results excerpts.

Boundary: Small, screened sample and supported setting; self-report is not metaphysical verification.

## S07

Mason, N. L., et al. (2021). [Spontaneous and deliberate creative cognition during and after psilocybin exposure](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8032715/). Translational Psychiatry 11, 209. DOI: [10.1038/s41398-021-01335-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41398-021-01335-5).

Inspection: Indexed methods and results; direct full-page access challenged.

Boundary: Task and time-specific results; no general intelligence or evolutionary conclusion.

## S08

Carhart-Harris, R. L.; Friston, K. J. (2019). [REBUS and the Anarchic Brain: Toward a Unified Model of the Brain Action of Psychedelics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6588209/). Pharmacological Reviews 71, 316–344. DOI: [10.1124/pr.118.017160](https://doi.org/10.1124/pr.118.017160).

Inspection: Abstract and theoretical formulation.

Boundary: Mechanistic model; not a settled account or proof that experiences reveal hidden reality.

## S09

Rodríguez Arce, J. M.; Winkelman, M. J. (2021). [Psychedelics, Sociality, and Human Evolution](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.729425/full). Frontiers in Psychology 12, 729425. DOI: [10.3389/fpsyg.2021.729425](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.729425).

Inspection: Full article available; hypothesis framing and evidence limitations inspected.

Boundary: Explicit hypothesis-and-theory article, not direct evidence of ancestral psychedelic selection.

## S10

Luhrmann, T. M., et al. (2021). [Sensing the presence of gods and spirits across cultures and faiths](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7865123/). PNAS 118(5), e2016649118. DOI: [10.1073/pnas.2016649118](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2016649118).

Inspection: Indexed abstract, methods and discussion; direct page challenged.

Boundary: Cross-cultural associations in reports; neither drug-origin proof nor metaphysical adjudication.

## S11

Muthukrishna, M.; Henrich, J. (2016). [Innovation in the collective brain](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4780534/). Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B 371, 20150192. DOI: [10.1098/rstb.2015.0192](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2015.0192).

Inspection: Article excerpts and author bibliographic record.

Boundary: Cultural-evolution account; not evidence of a psychedelic cause.

## S12

Forstmann, M., et al. (2020). [Transformative experience and social connectedness mediate the mood-enhancing effects of psychedelic use in naturalistic settings](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7007572/). PNAS 117, 2338–2346. DOI: [10.1073/pnas.1918477117](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1918477117).

Inspection: Article results and limitations excerpts.

Boundary: Observational association; mediation analysis does not establish experimental causation.

## S13

## S14

Indigenous Peyote Conservation Initiative (undated; accessed 2026-10-13). [Political and Social Issues](https://www.ipci.life/politicalandsocialissues). Community organization statement.

Inspection: Organization page inspected.

Boundary: Authoritative account of this organization’s position, not a voice for every Indigenous community.

## S15

Sharma, M., et al. (2023). [Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548). arXiv:2310.13548; primary research preprint (2023 version).

Inspection: Primary abstract and institutional research summary inspected.

Boundary: Evidence about tested models and tasks, not every present-day AI interaction.

## S16

Butler, M.; Jelen, L.; Rucker, J. (2022). [Expectancy in placebo-controlled trials of psychedelics: if so, so what?](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9481484/). Psychopharmacology. DOI: [10.1007/s00213-022-06221-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00213-022-06221-6).

Inspection: Article excerpts and full page available; Crossref author/title/year metadata checked.

Boundary: Methodological discussion; unblinding complicates attribution without making every finding worthless.
