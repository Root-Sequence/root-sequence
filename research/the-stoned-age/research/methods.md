# Method, limits and next evidence pass

Status: v0.1, AI-assisted targeted narrative synthesis, 2026-10-13. No original empirical research or systematic review. All source IDs resolve in [sources.md](sources.md); claims and revision triggers live in [claims.csv](claims.csv).

## Search record

Search surfaces: web search with primary journal, PMC, institutional and author records; Crossref bibliographic lookup; community organization website. Language primarily English. No date restriction. No exhaustive citation chaining. Searches were selected to address the requested project themes, not to estimate the total literature.

Query families actually used: Miller 2019 psychotropic ritual bundle; Arie Rosen Namdar Arad; Ren 2019 Pamirs; Guerra-Doce Menorca hair; Griffiths mystical-type experience; Mason creativity; Carhart-Harris Friston REBUS; Rodríguez Arce Winkelman evolution; Luhrmann spiritual presence; Muthukrishna Henrich collective brain; Forstmann connectedness; Carbonaro challenging experiences; expectancy psychedelic trials; IPCI political/social issues; Sharma sycophancy.

Inclusion: a traceable source with a specific role in a bounded claim or contrast. Preference for original studies, explicit theoretical papers and the relevant community's own statement. Exclusion from factual support: Reddit commentary, general encyclopedia summaries, commercial explanations, unsupported origin narratives and search results whose source could not be identified. These exclusions were editorial, not blinded screening. No PRISMA-style count is claimed.

Access: several PMC direct opens returned challenges even when indexed article passages were available; the Mason publisher page redirected to an inaccessible identity page. This reduced inspection depth. The register marks excerpt-level versus full-page access. “Full article available” does not mean independent critical appraisal or data reproduction. Crossref supplied metadata for nine of fourteen DOI records; five requests returned rate limits. The remaining records were checked against visible source records where available.

## What this first pass contributes

- Four cases separated by evidence type rather than arranged into an unsupported global origin story.
- A causal-chain critique that distinguishes possible exposure, measured effects, evolutionary transmission and explanatory comparison.
- Clear separation of subjective insight, task performance, personal meaning, metaphysical claims and decision authority.
- An AI analogy bounded to interpretation and corroboration, with its extension marked as a hypothesis.
- Shared source identifiers and revision triggers across the public-facing editions.

These are synthesis and communication contributions. No novelty finding, validated theory, comprehensive cultural history or causal estimate is claimed.

## Priority 1: full study extraction

Before upgrading v0.1 to a reviewed release, extract S01–S04 and S06–S07 in full. For archaeology: sample provenance, dates and uncertainty, preservation, analytes, controls, contamination, alternative uses, investigator interpretation and permissions. For experiments: population, sample size by analysis, assignment, exclusions, preregistration, blinding assessment, measures, effect estimates and uncertainty, attrition, adverse-event collection and conflicts of interest.

Do not turn abstract verification into a quality score. Record unknowns explicitly. Check corrections, retractions and later replications; this initial pass did not perform a comprehensive update or retraction audit. Obtain disciplinary review of the highest-leverage inferences. A source that cannot be inspected should support only the narrow statement that can be checked.

## Priority 2: cultural and religious scholarship

Commission or invite situated scholarship on one context at a time, with compensation and consent terms where applicable. Potential chapters: a carefully bounded Mazatec history; distinct Amazonian traditions and their changes; peyote conservation and sovereignty; textual arguments around Soma or Eleusis; non-drug religious experience. These are proposed topics, not completed chapters or claims that their drug identifications are settled.

Review categories with historians and anthropologists: myth, religion, ritual, medicine and altered state may be poor translations in some contexts. Do not extract secret knowledge or use a single reviewer as blanket cultural authorization. Record disagreements rather than replacing them with a single convenient voice.

## Priority 3: test narrower questions

Begin with a non-interventional literature appraisal of confidence versus accuracy and the generation versus evaluation of questions. Later, qualified researchers could examine whether interpretations remain corrigible across time and social contexts. Human-participant research requires a separate protocol, ethics process, safeguarding and appropriate expertise; none is authorized by this draft.

For AI, first define the outcome: agreement, factual accuracy, confidence change or willingness to revise. A fictional transcript audit can test a coding scheme, not establish real-world benefit or harm. Model outputs are not participant data. The project must not label an invented demonstration an experiment.

## Maintenance

Use one source register and one claims ledger. Edit Markdown/JSON sources, rebuild reader editions, and note which claims changed. Recheck evidence when a consequential update appears or before a new release; no automatic monitoring is scheduled. Retain stable source IDs even when records are corrected. Record retired claims rather than silently deleting their history.

A reviewer should be able to answer: what changed, what supports it, what could defeat it, and which editions now reflect it?
