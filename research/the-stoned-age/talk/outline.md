## Discussion and difficult questions

- “Are you recommending psychedelics?” No. The project is historical and epistemic inquiry, not a use or treatment guide.
- “Did drugs create religion?” This evidence does not establish that. Separate a documented practice from an origin claim.
- “Are spiritual experiences false?” The study of experiences does not alone settle their metaphysical interpretations.
- “Is AI conscious?” That is outside this comparison. We are examining interpretation and authority.
- “Why call it The Stoned Age?” Humor opens a door; the subtitle and visible evidence labels keep the scope honest.
