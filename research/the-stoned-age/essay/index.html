The Stoned Age

What happens after “I have figured it all out”?

Altered States, Human Curiosity, and the Long History of Making Meaning

An illustrated essay · 13 October 2026 · v0.1 · AI-assisted draft; review pending

How Drugs Explain the Universe is the provocation. How humans turn changed experience into meaning is the inquiry.

1. Please turn to page 666

Imagine a book so ambitious it explains consciousness before breakfast and invents religion by lunch. The joke is good. The research problem is better: what makes a strange experience feel like knowledge?

The first move is to separate four things. Something happened. Somebody interpreted it. Evidence may support that interpretation. Other people may be asked to act on it. None of these steps is automatic.

An experience can matter without being a message from the universe. An explanation can be elegant without being true. This is not an argument against wonder. It is a way to keep wonder from having to do every job at once.

2. Archaeology cannot interview the smoke

Four material windows help ground the story. Arad offers shrine residues; the Pamirs, funerary braziers; a South American bundle, evidence of psychotropic plants; Menorca, alkaloids in ancient hair. Each supports a bounded claim, with different limits. [S01, S02, S03, S04]

The seductive next sentence is “therefore, religion.” But a detected compound does not disclose a person's thoughts, establish a universal practice or identify the beginning of a tradition.

We should be able to say that evidence is important without making it total. A fragment is not a failed encyclopedia. It is a fragment worth understanding well.

3. The ape needs a better argument

“Psychedelics made us human” compresses several unproved steps. Were relevant ancestors exposed? What changed? How did that affect transmission or reproduction? Why does the explanation outperform alternatives?

A scholarly psychedelic-evolution hypothesis is a source of proposed mechanisms, not direct confirmation of the origin story. [S09] Possibility is the start of a question. It is not the end of one.

Nor is “drugs” one mechanism. Classic psychedelics, cannabis and other psychoactive compounds need separate treatment. [S05] The cultures using them need even more care. This essay does not reconstruct ceremonies or identify disputed ancient preparations.

4. A brilliant feeling needs a return visit

A controlled creativity study found that subjective insight and performance could move in different directions. [S07] That makes a useful distinction vivid: the experience of discovery and the evaluation of what was discovered are separate tasks.

Try this as a reading practice, without taking anything: put an exciting idea in one column and the evidence that would change your mind in another. The second column need not humiliate the first. It gives the idea somewhere to go.

Research can record enduring spiritual significance without confirming cosmic truth. [S06] A person may learn something important about what they value. There is no requirement that they also solve metaphysics.

5. The audience is part of the experience

Cultural models and individual absorption are associated with reports of spiritual presence. [S10] That gives us reason to investigate context, rather than treating meaning as a substance's fixed output.

Who listens? Who translates? Which version gets repeated? Who can disagree without losing their place in the group? These are our research questions. They do not assume that all traditions work alike.

A community can cherish an experience while declining to make it compulsory knowledge. A person can be heard without becoming an oracle. The distinction becomes especially important when a claim is used to decide what others must do.

6. Respect is part of the method

The Indigenous Peyote Conservation Initiative articulates priorities grounded in Indigenous cultural perspectives. [S14] An open research project does not acquire ownership of a tradition by finding it online.

Our illustrations show relationships and questions. They do not impersonate sacred art or reconstruct somebody else's ceremony. Future cultural chapters need appropriate authorship, sources and review.

This is also a history with room for distress, conflict and disappointed expectations. Meaning does not cancel harm. No reader needs to use a drug, disclose a personal experience or endorse a spiritual interpretation to take part.

7. The oracle has autocomplete

AI belongs here through a limited comparison: how a compelling interpretation acquires authority. Research on tested language models has documented sycophancy. [S15] A response that agrees beautifully is not a second independent witness.

We are not claiming that AI is intoxicated, conscious or pharmacologically comparable to a person. We are asking what happens when a tool helps make an interpretation sound finished.

This essay is AI-assisted. Its confidence must not outrun its sources either.

8. Keep the joke. Check the claim.

The interesting project survives the grand origin story failing. There are still histories to understand, experiences to hear, hypotheses to test and institutions to question.

Root Sequence is the umbrella for that work. It coordinates inquiry; it does not replace the fields or communities whose knowledge the inquiry depends on.

Start with the paper, inspect the source register, or use the talk to open a discussion. A good next edition will not merely be longer. It will know more clearly what it can say.
