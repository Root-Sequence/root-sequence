# Sources and Inspection Record

**Checked:** 2026-10-26, AI-assisted selected-source pass.<br>
**Scope:** The references below support the [synthesis](synthesis.md). This is neither a systematic review nor a completed historical adjudication. Primary documents, later judicial interpretations, institutional histories, and authors' arguments serve different purposes.

“Inspected” describes the retrieved text or image actually examined, not human author reading, independent authentication, agreement, or full-source appraisal. Search discovery alone is weaker and is named as such. Links were accessed during drafting; future availability is not guaranteed.

## Gaps that constrain the synthesis

The largest gaps are direct founding-era archival appraisal; department-specific patrol/police succession histories; Black testimony and scholarship beyond statutes and later courts; nation-specific Indigenous archives and contemporary historians; and care, disability, feminist, abolitionist, and anarchist scholarship beyond the two brief intellectual routes above. These gaps rule out a comprehensive origin story, a ranking of political systems, and a novelty claim for the Extended Hand formulation.

No historical massacre is converted into a playable reenactment here. New [simulation proposals](simulation-proposals.md) use fictional service settings and explicit assumptions. The [creative motifs](creative.md) have no evidential role.
