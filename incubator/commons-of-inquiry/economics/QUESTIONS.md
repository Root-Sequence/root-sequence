# Economics and Ownership Questions

A commons that only works for people with money is not enough.

This document does not yet choose a legal or financial model.

## Affordability

- What does it cost to participate for a day, weekend, month, or year?
- Can participation be free or subsidized when needed?
- Can people with irregular income participate?
- How are travel and caregiving costs handled?
- Are fellowships useful, or do they recreate prestige competition?

## Ownership

Possible models to investigate include:

- nonprofit ownership;
- cooperative ownership;
- community land trust;
- resident-owned housing cooperative;
- foundation / trust;
- split structures for land, operations, and programs;
- long-term lease instead of ownership;
- federated network with no central property.

The legal form should follow governance and material goals rather than become the ideology.

## Funding

Potential sources:

- resident contributions;
- grants;
- donations;
- aligned philanthropy;
- public arts / science / education funding;
- memberships;
- workshops or events;
- cooperative enterprises;
- endowment;
- shared equipment / studio programs.

For each source ask:

- What does the funder gain?
- What behavior does this select for?
- Does it push the commons toward measurable output?
- Can funding be withdrawn to control decisions?
- Does revenue depend on exploiting resident labor?
- Does the institution become inaccessible without constant growth?

## Labor

Track:

- domestic labor;
- maintenance;
- facilitation;
- administration;
- accessibility work;
- conflict mediation;
- technical infrastructure;
- event labor;
- emotional / relational labor.

Not all communal contribution needs wages, and not all labor can ethically be called volunteering. The boundary must be explicit and revisable.

## Risk

Who absorbs:

- vacancies;
- property damage;
- medical / disability-related interruptions;
- grant loss;
- maintenance shocks;
- legal costs;
- expansion failures?

A resilient commons should not depend on one resident or founder personally guaranteeing the institution forever.
