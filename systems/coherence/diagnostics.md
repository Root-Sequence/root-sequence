# Coherence Diagnostics

This method identifies mismatches between system assumptions and real-world conditions.

## Step 1: Identify assumptions

What must be true for this system to function?

Examples:
- users behave correctly
- systems remain available
- data is accurate
- communication is clear

## Step 2: Identify environment

What conditions actually exist?

Examples:
- human error
- adversarial actors
- outages
- incomplete information

## Step 3: Compare

Where do assumptions and reality diverge?

## Step 4: Identify dependence

Which parts of the system rely most heavily on these assumptions?

## Step 5: Evaluate impact

What happens when assumptions fail?

Does the system:
- degrade
- break
- cascade into failure

## Step 6: Identify hidden assumptions

What is assumed but not stated?
