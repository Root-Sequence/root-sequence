# Coherence Model

Coherence is the alignment between a system’s structure, its assumptions, and the reality it operates within.

## Core terms

An assumption is a condition the system expects to be true.

An environment is the actual context in which the system operates.

Alignment is the degree to which assumptions match reality.

Mismatch is a divergence between expectation and condition.

Incoherence occurs when a system relies on assumptions that do not hold.

## Patterns

Coherence is reduced when:

- systems assume stability in dynamic environments
- systems assume trust in adversarial conditions
- systems assume correctness in human processes
- systems depend on ideal conditions to function
- system behavior diverges from its intended design

## Properties

Coherence may be:

- explicit or implicit
- stable or drifting
- local or systemic
- visible or hidden

## Guiding questions

What does the system assume?

Do those assumptions match reality?

Under what conditions does the system fail?

What only works when nothing goes wrong?

Where does behavior diverge from design?

## Principle

Fragility often emerges where coherence is missing.
