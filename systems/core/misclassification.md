# Misclassification

**Document role:** Cross-domain analytical systems principle<br>
**Status:** Developing<br>
**Evidence boundary:** A disputed or inaccurate label does not automatically prove that a system is preserving itself. Function, effects, alternatives, and intent where claimed require evidence.<br>

Systems often label things incorrectly in order to preserve stability.

## Examples

- control -> "care"
- fear -> "respect"
- exploitation -> "opportunity"
- survival -> "choice"

## Core idea

Labels shape how systems are understood.

If a harmful dynamic is labeled as normal or positive, it becomes easier to repeat and reinforce.

## Implication

Understanding a system requires questioning:

- what is being called what?
- who benefits from that label?

Misclassification is often what allows harmful systems to persist without appearing harmful.
