# Feedback Loops

Systems reinforce the behaviors that sustain them.

## Core idea

A feedback loop occurs when:

- an action produces an outcome
- that outcome influences future actions

Over time, this creates patterns that stabilize or destabilize the system.

## Types

Reinforcing loops:
- amplify behavior
- can lead to growth or runaway effects

Balancing loops:
- counteract change
- can stabilize or suppress behavior

## Implication

Understanding a system requires identifying:

- what behaviors are being reinforced?
- what outcomes are feeding back into the system?

Feedback loops are what turn individual events into persistent patterns.
