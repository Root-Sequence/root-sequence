# Asymmetric Systems

Not all participants in a system play the same role.

Some systems shape conditions.
Others adapt to those conditions.

These roles are not equivalent.

## Core idea

In many systems:

- one side introduces constraints, pressure, or instability
- another side adapts in order to survive, respond, or stabilize

Treating both sides as equivalent removes the direction of influence and the origin of harm.

## Implication

Understanding a system requires asking:

- who is shaping the environment?
- who is responding to it?

Without this, analysis becomes flattened and accountability disappears.
