# Reinforcement

Systems do not stabilize around truth alone.

They stabilize around what gets repeated and reinforced.

## Core idea

A pattern becomes stable when:

- it is repeated
- and that repetition produces some form of reward, relief, or acceptance

Over time:

reaction -> repetition -> reinforcement -> belief -> system

## Compression and expansion

When a system encounters instability, it can respond in different ways.

Compression:
- reduces complexity
- lowers emotional or cognitive load
- stabilizes quickly

Expansion:
- increases awareness
- tolerates discomfort
- adapts more deeply over time

Compression often wins in the short term because it is easier to repeat and reinforce.

Expansion becomes stable only when it is supported and reinforced over time.

## Implication

Changing a system is not only about changing ideas.

It requires changing what gets repeated and reinforced.

## Key question

What patterns are currently being reinforced, and what patterns could be reinforced instead?
