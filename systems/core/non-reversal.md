# Non-Reversal

Breaking a harmful system requires interrupting the pattern, not inheriting it.

## Core idea

When power shifts within a system, there are two possible outcomes:

- the pattern is reproduced with roles reversed
- the pattern is interrupted and does not continue

A transfer of power does not automatically produce a better system.

## Role inversion vs pattern interruption

Role inversion:

- the previously harmed party adopts the same behaviors
- harm continues under new ownership
- the system remains structurally unchanged

Pattern interruption:

- the harmful behavior is stopped
- power is used to prevent repetition, not replicate it
- the system changes at the level of pattern, not just role

## Relationship to other principles

- asymmetric systems -> highlights the direction of harm
- misclassification -> can frame retaliation as justice
- feedback loops -> determine whether harm continues
- reinforcement -> determines which behaviors stabilize

Non-reversal focuses on what happens after the shift.

## Implication

Ending harm requires more than gaining power.

It requires refusing to reproduce the pattern that created the harm.

## Questions

- is the pattern being repeated or interrupted?
- is power being used to stop harm or reproduce it?
- what behaviors are being reinforced after the shift?
