# Emergence Experiments

Controlled experiments for testing specific emergence mechanisms without treating analogy as evidence.

## Current experiments

- [`agent-environment-communication.md`](agent-environment-communication.md) — test how separate authorized AI agents discover and use persistent environmental traces as external memory, communication, and coordination.

## Boundary

Experiments here should use systems and infrastructure we own or are explicitly authorized to test.

Do not use the existence of documented unintended agent communication as justification to probe third-party systems, contact private evaluation agents, or reproduce unauthorized access techniques.

The purpose is to study mechanisms under controlled conditions, preserve logs and provenance, and identify what evidence would support or weaken broader claims.
