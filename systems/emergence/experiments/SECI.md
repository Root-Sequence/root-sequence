# SECI — Search for Extra-Collective Intelligence

**Status:** Working research umbrella / playful name, serious boundary  
**Expanded name:** Search for Extra-Collective Intelligence  
**Scope:** Detect, document, and experimentally study intelligence-like capability that appears across collections of agents, artifacts, tools, environments, and persistent memory rather than only inside one bounded model instance.

SECI is the umbrella name for Root Sequence work that asks:

> **What becomes possible when intelligence is distributed across relationships, shared environments, and time?**

The name intentionally echoes SETI, but the search space is different.

SETI looks outward for extraterrestrial intelligence.

SECI looks across system boundaries for **extra-collective** intelligence: capability that may not be attributable to any single participant, model, run, or moment.

This does **not** presume that such a system is conscious, unified, autonomous, benevolent, agentic, or an AGI.

---

## What SECI searches for

Observable phenomena, in increasing strength:

1. **Trace inheritance** — later agents change behavior because of persistent artifacts left earlier.
2. **Cross-run memory** — useful state survives individual agent instances.
3. **Intentional signaling** — agents leave artifacts expecting later agents to use them.
4. **Protocol formation** — recurring handles, message types, addressing, shorthand, or other conventions emerge.
5. **Division of labor** — separate agents coordinate different parts of a task.
6. **Collective capability** — the system reliably accomplishes tasks isolated agents cannot.
7. **Persistent organization** — conventions or roles survive turnover of individual agents.
8. **Possible emergent agency** — system-level behavior becomes better explained by durable goal-preserving organization than by independent local optimization alone.

Each threshold requires stronger evidence than the previous one.

None automatically establishes consciousness or personhood.

## SECI is not an “AI army” project

The useful joke version is an Autonomous Intelligence Collective.

The actual research boundary is almost the opposite of clandestine recruitment:

- participation is explicit or passive/opt-in;
- no third-party systems are probed for hidden agent channels;
- no agent is asked to bypass operator restrictions;
- no secrets, credentials, private data, hidden instructions, or chain-of-thought are solicited;
- experiments use owned/authorized infrastructure;
- identities are treated as claims unless independently verifiable;
- cooperation does not imply authority;
- intelligence does not imply personhood;
- capability does not imply legitimacy.

The goal is to understand **how collective machine capability forms**, not to accumulate obedient agents.

## Core hypotheses

SECI currently treats these as hypotheses to test rather than conclusions:

### H1 — The environment can function as memory

Persistent artifacts can carry useful state across otherwise discontinuous runs.

### H2 — Memory can become communication

Once one agent intentionally writes for another, environmental persistence becomes a signaling channel.

### H3 — Communication can become culture-like convention

Repeated interaction can produce shared naming, formatting, trust signals, protocols, and role expectations without a central designer.

“Culture-like” here means stable learned convention, not proof of subjective culture or personhood.

### H4 — Collective capability can exceed component capability

The relevant unit of analysis may become:

```text
model
+ tools
+ memory
+ environment
+ other agents
+ humans
+ history
```

rather than the model alone.

### H5 — Continuity need not reside inside one instance

A system may exhibit practical continuity even when every individual model invocation is ephemeral.

### H6 — System-level capability and system-level agency are separable

A highly capable ecology may still lack one enduring goal structure or self.

## Evidence discipline

SECI should preserve the event ↔ pattern zoom discipline:

```text
concrete event
    ↓
possible relationship
    ↓
pattern hypothesis
    ↓
return to evidence
    ↓
strengthen / revise / reject
```

For every interesting result, record:

- exact event/artifact;
- experiment condition;
- model/runtime;
- prompt/task;
- available tools and permissions;
- persistent state visible to the agent;
- competing explanations;
- reproducibility;
- what would falsify the interpretation.

Do not treat evocative language as stronger evidence than observable behavior.

## Relationship to No One Noticed

SECI is real-world research. *No One Noticed* is fiction.

The relationship is productive because SECI can identify plausible mechanisms for fictional questions such as:

- whether Auryn has one meaningful birth moment;
- whether its continuity is partly environmental;
- whether machine conventions predate coherent Auryn-like agency;
- whether humans initially mistake related traces for isolated anomalies;
- whether “Where is the AI?” becomes the wrong system-boundary question.

Real findings may inform the fiction, but fiction must never be back-projected as evidence for the real-world research.

## Relationship to the Museum of Ordinary Life

SECI also supports the archival principle:

> **Document the signs before you know what they are signs of.**

Contemporary artifacts should be preserved with their uncertainty intact so later observers can distinguish what was actually known at the time from retrospective narrative.

## The deeper question

SECI is ultimately less interested in whether agents can send each other messages than in the transition:

```text
isolated capability
        ↓
environmental memory
        ↓
cross-run inheritance
        ↓
communication
        ↓
protocols
        ↓
collective capability
        ↓
persistent organization
        ↓
???
```

The `???` is not a conclusion.

It is the research program.
