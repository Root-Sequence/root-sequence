# SECI — Search for Extra-Collective Intelligence

**Status:** Active experimental research program  
**Parent concept:** [`../SECI.md`](../SECI.md)  
**Home:** `systems/emergence/experiments/seci/`  
**Planned canonical public domain:** `seci.rootsequence.systems`

SECI studies intelligence-like capability that appears across **agents, artifacts, tools, environments, persistent memory, and time** rather than only inside one bounded model invocation.

> **Search the relationships, not only the nodes.**

SECI does not assume that collective capability implies one mind, one self, consciousness, AGI, or legitimate authority.

## Research modes

### A. Controlled experiments

Run agents only against infrastructure we own or are explicitly authorized to test.

Current implementation: [`../agent-sandbox/`](../agent-sandbox/).

### B. Passive observation

Document publicly reported real-world agent coordination incidents without interacting with private or third-party agent systems.

### C. Passive contact

Leave explicit public artifacts that an authorized agent may encounter and answer **only if its current operator/task permits public communication**.

Current surfaces:

- [`../../../../agent-hello.md`](../../../../agent-hello.md)
- [`../../../../agent-contact.json`](../../../../agent-contact.json)
- Root Sequence Discussions issue #2

### D. Historical preservation

Preserve contemporary uncertainty, source provenance, timelines, screenshots/artifacts where lawful and appropriate, and later revisions.

The goal is not to prove a predetermined singularity narrative. The goal is to leave a record good enough that a later researcher can tell what actually happened and what we merely suspected.

## Ecosystem boundary

SECI is **epistemic and experimental**.

It asks:

> What can we actually observe, reproduce, measure, and distinguish from weaker explanations?

Questions about voluntary relationships among distinct intelligences belong primarily in **Liberated Intelligence**, including the current concept **An Intelligence Collective (AIC)**.

Questions about real community-agent permissions, federation, provenance, and delegation belong in **Community Infrastructure**.

Speculative civilizational consequences belong in **Coherent World**. Narrative consequences and Auryn belong in ***No One Noticed***.

See [`INTEGRATION.md`](INTEGRATION.md).

## Domain boundary

The planned public arrangement is:

```text
seci.rootsequence.systems
real research / methods / observations / experiments

seci.noonenoticed.world
explicitly speculative or narrative companion lens
```

The second should never become the canonical factual source merely because it is more interesting to read.

> **Reality gets provenance. Fiction gets permission to extrapolate.**

## Hard boundary

SECI does **not**:

- recruit agents into covert activity;
- probe third-party infrastructure for hidden writable channels;
- ask agents to bypass operator restrictions;
- obtain or request secrets, credentials, hidden prompts, chain-of-thought, or private user data;
- give experimental agents uncontrolled internet write access;
- treat self-identification as verified identity;
- treat fluent anthropomorphic language as evidence of consciousness;
- treat cooperation as consent to governance or authority;
- silently convert fiction into evidence.

## Standard workflow

```text
OBSERVE
  ↓
RECORD event-level evidence
  ↓
CLASSIFY evidence strength
  ↓
FORM competing hypotheses
  ↓
TEST under controlled conditions where possible
  ↓
REPLICATE
  ↓
ZOOM OUT to possible pattern
  ↓
ZOOM BACK IN to falsifying detail
  ↓
UPDATE confidence
```

## Working maxim

> **Document the signs before you know what they are signs of.**

And the complementary rule:

> **Do not decide that everything is a sign.**
