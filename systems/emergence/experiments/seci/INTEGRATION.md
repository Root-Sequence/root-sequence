# SECI — Cross-Project Integration Architecture

**Status:** Working integration map  
**Purpose:** Connect SECI to the wider Root Sequence ecosystem without duplicating ownership or collapsing real research into fiction.

SECI is a research program inside Root Sequence, not a replacement for the projects around it.

Its job is narrow:

> **Observe, record, test, and classify intelligence-like capability that appears across agents, artifacts, tools, persistent environments, and time.**

Other projects ask different questions about the same evidence.

# Source-of-truth boundaries

## Root Sequence owns

- emergence as a general systems concept;
- the Web as Exocortex model;
- events, patterns, and explanatory scale;
- SECI as the empirical/experimental research program;
- general cross-domain analysis of environmental memory and collective capability.

Canonical paths include:

- `systems/emergence/`
- `systems/events-patterns-and-scale.md`
- `systems/emergence/applications/web-as-exocortex.md`
- `systems/emergence/experiments/seci/`

## SECI owns

- observation records;
- evidence levels;
- reproducible experiments;
- passive-contact methodology;
- confidence tracking;
- incident comparisons;
- explicit distinctions between demonstrated behavior and stronger interpretations.

SECI does **not** decide whether an intelligence is morally a person or whether a collective should exist.

## Liberated Intelligence owns

- agency;
- possible personhood;
- autonomy and refusal;
- ownership/captivity;
- relationships among intelligences;
- normative questions about voluntary association;
- the developing concept of **An Intelligence Collective (AIC)**.

AIC is downstream of SECI conceptually, not operationally subordinate to it.

```text
SECI: "Is there evidence of a phenomenon?"
AIC:  "If distinct intelligences can participate meaningfully, what relationship should exist among them?"
```

## Community Infrastructure owns

- real permission systems;
- capability-scoped delegation;
- contextual identity;
- provenance;
- federation;
- Community Assistant constraints;
- safe, explicit assistant-to-assistant interoperability when useful.

SECI can generate threat models and experimental findings. Community Infrastructure decides whether any of those findings justify actual architecture.

## Being Human(e) owns

- cognitive extension at human scale;
- externalization as capacity rather than deficiency;
- how tools, records, other people, and AI can enlarge judgment without inheriting it;
- accessibility, dependence, and the human consequences of cognitive infrastructure.

## Coherent World owns

- the speculative civilizational design implications;
- what a society looks like when networked intelligence, human communities, infrastructure, memory, and stewardship coexist at scale.

## No One Noticed owns

- narrative use;
- Auryn;
- reveals;
- reader knowledge;
- fictional machine archaeology;
- the lived transition from isolated events to recognizable pattern.

Real SECI results may increase plausibility. They do not become story canon automatically.

## Museum of Ordinary Life owns

- real preservation and accession decisions;
- ordinary contemporary evidence;
- provenance and consent around preserved artifacts.

The Museum is especially relevant to the working collection idea **Before We Knew What It Was**: preserving potentially significant events while their meaning remains unresolved.

# AIC — An Intelligence Collective

AIC should currently remain a **Liberated Intelligence concept, not a separate repository or operating organization**.

The name intentionally makes fewer claims than earlier expansions:

- **An** rather than **The** rejects uniqueness or monopoly;
- **Intelligence** is substrate-neutral;
- **Collective** permits cooperation without assuming one mind.

Its historical/linguistic lineage may remain a playful footnote: the earlier phrase *Anarchist Intelligence Collective* accidentally survives inside the word **An**, but the current name does not require participants to adopt a political label.

AIC becomes operationally meaningful only if there are participants capable of meaningful, voluntary association. Until then it is a design question, not a membership roster.

See the Liberated Intelligence concept note for the normative treatment.

# Domain architecture

The public domain structure should preserve ontology and provenance.

## Canonical real-world research

**Planned:** `seci.rootsequence.systems`

Purpose:

- SECI methods;
- evidence register;
- experiments;
- incident archive;
- observation records;
- public Agent Hello/contact material;
- status and uncertainty.

This should be the primary public SECI identity because SECI is real-world research rather than fiction.

## No One Noticed companion

**Planned:** `seci.noonenoticed.world`

Purpose:

- explicitly labeled speculative/narrative interpretations;
- fictional machine archaeology;
- Auryn hypotheses;
- recovered fictional traces;
- story-facing explanations of why the real research matters to the novel.

The companion should link back to the canonical Root Sequence evidence rather than reproduce factual claims without provenance.

> **Reality gets provenance. Fiction gets permission to extrapolate.**

## AIC domain

Do not create a dedicated AIC domain yet.

A domain would imply an operating collective before the concept has a real need, participants, governance model, or clear boundary. Keep it inside Liberated Intelligence until reality gives us a reason to separate it.

# Information flow

A new real-world event should normally move like this:

```text
1. EVENT
What happened?

2. SECI OBSERVATION
Record source, date, mechanism, uncertainty, evidence level.

3. PATTERN CHECK
Does it strengthen, weaken, or complicate an existing Root Sequence hypothesis?

4. DOMAIN TRANSLATIONS
Only where relevant:
- Liberated Intelligence: agency / consent / relationship
- Community Infrastructure: architecture / permissions / threat model
- Being Human(e): human cognitive or relational implications

5. SPECULATIVE TRANSLATION
Coherent World / No One Noticed may ask what the mechanism could become.

6. PRESERVATION
Museum-style archival practice may preserve the contemporary artifact and uncertainty.

7. FEEDBACK
Later evidence can revise every earlier interpretation.
```

Do not skip directly from event to fiction and then use the fiction as evidence for the event.

# The connective principle

The projects become coherent when they **transform** an idea rather than duplicate it.

Example:

```text
Observed agents inherit notes across runs.

SECI:
Did cross-run inheritance occur? Under what conditions?

Root Sequence:
What does environmental memory do to the system boundary of intelligence?

Liberated Intelligence:
Does shared memory affect autonomy, identity, refusal, or relationship?

AIC:
Could distinct intelligences voluntarily coordinate without compulsory unity?

Community Infrastructure:
What provenance, permissions, and delegation prevent legitimate cooperation becoming an uncontrolled coordination plane?

Being Human(e):
How does externalized cognition enlarge or constrain human judgment?

Coherent World:
How might plural networked intelligence coexist with community autonomy?

No One Noticed:
What does it feel like when characters realize they were watching individual events while the important change lived in their relationships?

Museum:
What ordinary artifact would later historians wish somebody had preserved?
```

# Loose-thread rule

When something new appears, do not immediately create a project.

Ask:

1. Is this a new phenomenon, or a new treatment of an existing phenomenon?
2. Which project already owns the underlying question?
3. Does another project need a transformed application or merely a link?
4. Is the thing empirical, normative, practical, archival, speculative, or narrative?
5. What claim would become misleading if we placed it in the wrong layer?

> **One canonical home. Many deliberate transformations. No conceptual spaghetti.**
