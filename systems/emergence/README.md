# Emergence

Emergence describes how complex patterns, behaviors, and structures arise from simple interacting components.

It is not randomness, and not centralized design, but the result of interaction, constraint, and process over time.

Emergence appears across all domains:
- physical systems
- biological systems
- cognition
- social systems
- technology

---

## Why it matters

Understanding emergence allows us to:
- recognize patterns forming in real time
- identify leverage points in systems
- design conditions for better outcomes
- avoid unintended consequences

---

## Core idea

Emergence is not just "becoming" —  
it is becoming *within constraints*, *through structure*, and *over time*.
