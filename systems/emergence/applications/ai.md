# Emergence in AI Systems

## Summary

AI systems exhibit emergent behavior when capabilities arise that were not explicitly programmed.

---

## Where emergence appears

- large language models
- neural networks
- multi-agent systems
- reinforcement learning

---

## Characteristics

- unexpected capabilities
- pattern generalization
- behavior not directly traceable to code

---

## Drivers

- scale
- data diversity
- interaction complexity
- training dynamics

---

## Risks

- unpredictable behavior
- misalignment
- unintended capabilities
- feedback amplification

---

## Constraints Matter

AI behavior is shaped by:
- training data
- architecture
- objective functions
- guardrails

---

## Design Implications

You cannot fully specify behavior.

But you can:
- shape training environments
- define constraints
- monitor feedback loops
- limit harmful emergence

---

## Connection to Root Sequence

AI is not just programmed.  
It is grown.
