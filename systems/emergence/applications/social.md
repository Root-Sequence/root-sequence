# Emergence in Social Systems

## Summary

Social systems are emergent.  
They are not designed in full, but arise from interactions between individuals, norms, and constraints.

---

## Key Dynamics

### Local interactions
- conversations
- behaviors
- decisions

### Feedback loops
- social reinforcement
- reputation
- norms

### Constraints
- laws
- culture
- access to resources

---

## Patterns that emerge

- communities
- institutions
- power structures
- inequality
- culture

---

## Risks

- harmful norms can emerge naturally
- power can centralize without intent
- exclusion can become self-reinforcing

---

## Design Implications

You cannot fully control social systems.

But you can:
- shape constraints
- enable positive feedback loops
- reduce harmful incentives
- create safe boundaries

---

## Example: Liberation Mass

Questions to explore:
- what constraints produce safe interaction?
- how do we prevent harmful dynamics from emerging?
- how do we support trust and mutual aid?

---

## Key Insight

Social outcomes are not just chosen.  
They emerge from conditions.
