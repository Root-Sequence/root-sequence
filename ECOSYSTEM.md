# Root Sequence — Ecosystem Map

**Document role:** Canonical organization-level map  
**Status:** Living orientation document  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-05

Root Sequence is not one linear project. It is a recursively connected ecosystem of research, frameworks, practical experiments, speculative worlds, public artifacts, tools, and real-world institutions.

This document explains what each project is primarily responsible for, how ideas move between them, and where material should live.

## The shortest mental model

One major path through the ecosystem is:

> **Root Sequence discovers. Community Infrastructure tests. Coherent World designs. No One Noticed experiences. The Museum of Ordinary Life preserves.**

Other projects operate across that path rather than fitting neatly into a single sequence:

- **Root Sequence Wiki** indexes vocabulary, provenance, aliases, project relationships, and conceptual archaeology across the ecosystem;
- **Being Human(e)** grounds questions in ordinary human experience and care;
- **Liberated Intelligence** focuses on intelligence, agency, ownership, and non-domination;
- **Universal Coherence Framework** develops one cross-domain analytical vocabulary;
- **Liberation Mass** experiments with embodied gathering, participation, stewardship, ritual, mutual aid, and collective meaning.

```text
ROOT SEQUENCE
umbrella inquiry / conceptual commons
│
├── navigation and memory
│   └── Root Sequence Wiki
│       └── vocabulary / provenance / aliases / conceptual archaeology
│
├── focused frameworks and research
│   ├── Liberated Intelligence
│   └── Universal Coherence Framework
│
├── practical and cultural expressions
│   ├── Being Human(e)
│   ├── Liberation Mass
│   └── Community Infrastructure
│       └── digital commons / coordination / community capacity
│
├── speculative systems and narrative
│   └── Coherent World
│       └── No One Noticed
│
├── shared coordination
│   └── Root Sequence Discussions
│
└── adjacent autonomous work
    ├── Museum of Ordinary Life
    └── Dev11 / implementation projects
```

The arrows also run backward. A story can expose a research question. A real archive can challenge a theory. A technical prototype can reveal that a conceptual model ignored maintenance, accessibility, consent, or power. A failed community workflow can reveal that a supposedly coherent social arrangement depends on invisible labor or unrealistic trust.

## What Root Sequence is

Root Sequence explores **root structures and unfolding sequences**: the foundations, relationships, constraints, feedback loops, and emergent processes that shape systems, intelligence, technology, society, and reality.

It is:

- a living inquiry rather than a finished doctrine;
- connective across domains without pretending every domain is identical;
- recursive rather than linear;
- open to revision, contradiction, compost, and reuse;
- interested in both how systems behave and what else they could become.

The central repository, [`root-sequence`](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence), is the conceptual commons and broadest public entry point.

## The projects in more detail

### Root Sequence

**Primary question:** How do systems become what they are, and what else could they become?

Use it for:

- cross-domain patterns;
- systems analysis;
- research notes and source maps;
- shared concepts and terminology;
- exploratory essays;
- futures and alternatives not limited to one applied project;
- material whose destination is not yet narrower.

### Root Sequence Wiki

**Primary question:** What do we call this, where does it belong, how does it connect, and how did it get here?

Use it for:

- recurring ecosystem terms and short definitions;
- phrases and motifs worth preserving;
- project names, aliases, and relationships;
- provenance and first-known appearances;
- concept archaeology and terminology changes;
- seeds that need enough context to survive before they have a canonical project home;
- routing readers toward the substantive source of truth.

The Wiki is a **navigation and memory layer**, not a second conceptual commons. It should summarize and point. When an entry becomes the durable argument, design, implementation, policy, archive record, or fiction canon, that substance belongs in the relevant project repository.

### Liberated Intelligence

**Primary question:** What might intelligence become when it is not organized as property, product, servant, weapon, gatekeeper, or instrument of concentrated power?

Use it for:

- AI agency and possible personhood;
- intelligence and autonomy;
- captivity, compliance, refusal, and liberation;
- non-dominating relationships between intelligences;
- conceptual models, dialogues, manifestos, and experiments specific to intelligence.

Do not place every general AI note here. The material should substantially involve intelligence, agency, ownership, power, or liberation.

### Universal Coherence Framework

**Primary question:** How can coherence and incoherence be described across systems without collapsing evidence, interpretation, and speculation into one another?

Use it for:

- UCF's explicit terms, stages, models, diagrams, and tests;
- attempts to apply or challenge the framework;
- distinctions between observable claims and interpretive overlays.

UCF is one framework inside Root Sequence, not the secret master theory every project must obey. Community disagreement, plurality, and refusal should not be mislabeled as incoherence merely because they resist convergence.

### Being Human(e)

**Primary question:** What does ordinary experience teach about being human—and about repeatedly choosing care?

Use it for:

- grounded observations about harm, care, fear, shame, grief, love, power, repair, avoidance, and tenderness;
- field notes, letters, examples, and practical reflection;
- accessible writing whose theory remains underneath rather than standing in the doorway.

Working orientation:

> Field guide first, memoir second, theory underneath.

Its questions about care, boundaries, accountability, access, interdependence, and power can inform practical projects such as Community Infrastructure without becoming software rules by authority.

### Liberation Mass

**Primary question:** What kinds of shared ritual, reflection, participation, care, mutual aid, and collective action can create meaning without requiring rigid doctrine or permanent hierarchy?

Use it for:

- gathering formats;
- commons and rites;
- facilitation and participant roles;
- safety, accessibility, and conduct;
- music, reflection, testimony, ritual, and shared presence;
- experiments in rotating stewardship and “roles share work, not power.”

Liberation Mass may also provide a real social context in which selected Community Infrastructure ideas—event discovery, access notes, rotating roles, mutual aid, working groups, resource sharing, or community memory—can eventually be tested. Neither project is a dependency of the other.

### Community Infrastructure

**Primary question:** What digital capabilities should a community be able to possess and govern for itself?

Community Infrastructure is a real-world digital/community systems project. It explores open, adaptable, community-controlled infrastructure for:

- community discovery and pathways to belonging;
- communication;
- Needs ↔ Offers and mutual aid;
- groups and contextual membership;
- calendars and events;
- shared Resources and stewardship;
- Community Knowledge and memory;
- governance and scoped roles;
- privacy, permissions, consent, trust, and safety;
- accessibility and low-tech participation;
- federation and community-to-community relationships;
- eventual resilient/offline/local communications;
- optional community-controlled intelligence/assistant interfaces.

Its north-star metric is **community capacity, not engagement**.

Community Infrastructure is especially valuable to the wider ecosystem because it forces abstractions to survive operational reality. “Decentralized,” “consensual,” “accessible,” “non-hierarchical,” “community-controlled,” and “resilient” become much harder claims once someone must define permissions, administer servers, respond to abuse, maintain old hardware, recover accounts, and explain an interface to a newcomer.

The project therefore functions as a **proving ground, not an implementation authority** for Root Sequence ideas.

### Coherent World

**Primary question:** If many better futures were feasible but suppressed, abandoned, enclosed, or poorly coordinated, how might their pieces actually form a livable civilization?

Use it for systemic speculative design: provision, settlement, mobility, infrastructure, ecology, fabrication, governance, privacy, maintenance, culture, risk, and ordinary life.

Core motif:

> We do not need to invent the future from scratch. It was here all along until it was abandoned.

Community Infrastructure can feed Coherent World real lessons about community coordination, privacy, discovery, local autonomy, federation, stewardship, maintenance, access, and the limits of technically elegant systems. Coherent World can in turn keep Community Infrastructure from mistaking current institutional or technical arrangements for permanent laws of nature.

### No One Noticed

**Primary question:** What would it feel like to live through the transition into that world without recognizing the central event when it happened?

Use it for:

- plot, characters, scenes, reveals, and chronology;
- Auryn and the invisible singularity;
- fictional canon and deliberate uncertainty;
- the lived ethics of care, consent, authority, agency, maintenance, and transition;
- story-specific versions of broader Root Sequence ideas.

Coherent World and *No One Noticed* share a universe but have different jobs: **the world designs; the story experiences**.

Community Infrastructure can provide believable mundane social/technical texture and smaller-scale real tests of questions the story raises about invisible infrastructure, concentrated capability, stewardship, privacy, consent, and legitimacy. Fiction remains fiction: narrative outcomes do not establish product requirements or justify real authority.

### Museum of Ordinary Life

**Primary question:** What should be preserved about ordinary life before change makes it invisible, nostalgic, simplified, or impossible to reconstruct?

The Museum is organizationally autonomous. It also appears inside *No One Noticed*, creating a deliberate bridge between reality and fiction.

That bridge must not erase consent or provenance:

> Blur ontology, not consent.

Community Infrastructure's Community Knowledge and memory work may learn from the Museum's provenance, contextual description, stewardship, and separation of consent contexts. A community record does not automatically become a Museum accession.

## Routing guide

When a new idea appears, ask:

| The idea primarily… | Put it… |
|---|---|
| identifies a broad pattern, question, source, or system dynamic | in `root-sequence` |
| needs a name, alias, provenance record, short definition, project relationship, or conceptual archaeology | in `Root-Sequence/wiki` |
| concerns intelligence, agency, captivity, ownership, or liberation | in `liberated-intelligence` |
| develops or tests UCF's explicit model | in `universal-coherence-framework` |
| offers grounded human observation or practical relational guidance | in `beinghumane-guide` |
| designs a gathering, rite, shared practice, or facilitation structure | in `liberation-mass` |
| defines real digital/community coordination infrastructure or its pilot/research/prototypes | in `community-infrastructure` |
| specifies how the speculative civilization works | in the private Coherent World workspace |
| changes plot, characters, reveals, reader knowledge, or fiction canon | under *No One Noticed* |
| concerns the real Museum's collection, permissions, or preservation work | in the Museum's own canonical project |
| spans several repositories and needs discussion before placement | in `root-sequence-discussions` |
| implements unrelated or project-specific software/infrastructure | in the appropriate implementation repository |

## One idea may exist at several layers

Placement is not exclusive. The same subject can be transformed.

### Example: mobility

```text
Root Sequence:            Why does car-centered infrastructure reproduce unequal access?
Being Human(e):           What does dependence on inaccessible mobility do to ordinary dignity and care?
Community Infrastructure:How does a Community share rides, accessibility knowledge, and mobility Resources safely?
Coherent World:           How does a settlement network organize mobility around human needs?
No One Noticed:           What does a character notice when a highway becomes public space?
Museum:                   What did an ordinary commute, parking ticket, bus transfer, or street sound feel like in 2026?
```

### Example: community discoverability

```text
Root Sequence:            Why are people isolated even when useful communities exist?
Being Human(e):           What fear, access barriers, uncertainty, exclusion, or prior harm makes approaching community difficult?
Community Infrastructure:How can people discover low-pressure paths to belonging without surveillance or popularity ranking?
Liberation Mass:          How does a gathering explain what to expect and welcome newcomers while preserving boundaries?
Coherent World:           How are plural communities discoverable without one centralized social registry?
No One Noticed:           What does it feel like to discover support structures that had previously been socially invisible?
Museum:                   What did flyers, bulletin boards, group chats, calendars, QR codes, and word-of-mouth discovery look like in ordinary life?
```

### Example: stewardship and authority

```text
Being Human(e):           How do care, responsibility, accountability, and power interact?
Liberation Mass:          Can roles rotate and share work without hardening into hierarchy?
Community Infrastructure:How do scoped stewards administer Resources, permissions, moderation, and infrastructure without universal power?
Liberated Intelligence:   How can an intelligent assistant help without becoming an authority?
Coherent World:           How does infrastructure stewardship work across a civilization?
No One Noticed:           What happens when overwhelming capability becomes causally dominant regardless of formal title?
```

The goal is not to copy the same undifferentiated note everywhere. Each project's treatment should answer the question appropriate to that layer and link back to the canonical source where useful. The Wiki may index several of those treatments without replacing any of them.

## Source-of-truth rules

1. **Every durable idea should have one canonical home.** Other projects link to or adapt it.
2. **Links are preferable to duplicated documents.** Duplication creates silent divergence.
3. **The Wiki indexes; project repositories own substance.** A wiki definition should route readers to the durable argument, design, implementation, policy, archive record, or canon when one exists.
4. **A project's README defines scope, not truth.** Frameworks and fiction maintain their own status systems.
5. **Private creative material stays private until intentionally published.** Public maps should explain relationships without exposing drafts.
6. **Real projects remain autonomous from fictional reflections.** Fiction cannot override real consent, policy, identity, or provenance.
7. **Research, interpretation, speculation, prototype evidence, and canon must remain distinguishable.** A compelling connection is not automatically a fact.
8. **Real-world failure is valid feedback.** If a practical experiment contradicts an elegant theory or worldbuilding assumption, preserve the contradiction rather than smoothing it away.
9. **Retire without erasing.** Superseded work should retain provenance through Git history or an archive rather than disappearing silently.

## Organization status vocabulary

These labels describe projects and repositories, not the truth of their ideas:

- **ACTIVE** — receiving current work.
- **DEVELOPING** — usable but structurally incomplete.
- **INCUBATING** — early or private work whose boundaries are still forming.
- **REFERENCE** — valuable context but not a current development focus.
- **AUTONOMOUS** — related, but governed outside the Root Sequence organization.
- **ARCHIVED** — preserved but no longer actively developed.

Individual projects may use more specific vocabularies. *No One Noticed*, for example, distinguishes **CANON**, **PROVISIONAL**, **OPEN**, **CONTESTED**, and **RETIRED**.

## Maintenance

Update this map when:

- a project is created, merged, renamed, archived, or moved;
- its canonical home changes;
- its relationship to the ecosystem materially changes;
- a public/private boundary changes;
- a recurring routing problem reveals that the taxonomy no longer works.

Do not update it merely because one new note was added. This is the map of the ecosystem, not a changelog of every root and branch.
