# 🔮 Futures — Trajectories and Alternatives

*Where systems may go, which paths are being blocked, and what can be practiced before the future becomes inevitable-looking history.*

This folder explores possible trajectories: collapse, authoritarian consolidation, adaptation, recovery, commons, abundance, resistance, and liberatory alternatives.

Futures are not predictions. They are structured possibilities built from present conditions, historical evidence, system dynamics, imagination, and uncertainty.

## Purpose

Use this folder to:

- map trajectories from present system conditions;
- distinguish prediction, scenario, preference, warning, and fiction;
- examine how power determines which futures receive resources and legitimacy;
- recover suppressed and abandoned possibilities without romanticizing them;
- study collapse without turning suffering into aesthetic scenery;
- develop resilience without normalizing institutional abandonment;
- explore abundance without ignoring ecology, maintenance, care, conflict, or unequal access;
- connect alternatives already practiced by people and communities;
- identify transition conditions rather than presenting finished utopias;
- prototype questions that Coherent World and other projects can treat more concretely.

## Futures are plural

Avoid framing the future as one track:

```text
current trend → inevitable endpoint
```

A better model is a field:

```text
conditions
+ power
+ constraints
+ choices
+ conflict
+ accidents
+ feedback
+ ecological limits
+ memory
+ imagination
→ branching trajectories
```

Some branches are more probable than others. Some actors possess far greater power to select among them. Some possibilities have already been damaged or foreclosed. Plurality does not mean every future is equally reachable.

## Status language

Futures documents should identify their role:

- **TRAJECTORY** — continuation or interaction of observed conditions;
- **SCENARIO** — internally structured possibility explored without prediction;
- **WARNING** — outcome the project wants to make easier to recognize or prevent;
- **PROPOSAL** — arrangement worth developing or testing;
- **RECOVERY** — abandoned or suppressed possibility being re-examined;
- **SPECULATION** — possibility extending beyond current evidence;
- **FICTION BRIDGE** — idea prepared for transformation by Coherent World or *No One Noticed*;
- **OPEN** — unresolved question or incompatible possibilities.

Normative preference must not be disguised as forecast confidence.

## Core questions

```text
WHAT CONDITIONS PRODUCE THIS FUTURE?

WHAT POWER RELATIONS SELECT IT?

WHO BENEFITS / WHO BEARS COSTS?

WHAT MATERIAL AND ECOLOGICAL LIMITS APPLY?

WHAT ALTERNATIVES ALREADY EXIST?

WHAT PREVENTS THEM FROM SURVIVING OR SCALING?

WHAT MAINTENANCE DOES THE FUTURE REQUIRE?

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN SYSTEMS FAIL?

WHO CAN REFUSE OR EXIT?

WHAT NEW DOMINATION RISKS APPEAR?

WHAT EVIDENCE SUPPORTS THE SCENARIO?

WHAT WOULD MAKE IT MORE OR LESS PLAUSIBLE?

WHAT CAN BE PRACTICED NOW WITHOUT PRETENDING THE TRANSITION IS COMPLETE?
```
